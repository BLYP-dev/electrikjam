<p>This is the week we finally get to see the Jackson 2021 Artist models, and some of them are exactly what you expect. But Jackson has a few tricks up their sleeve this year. Today we take a look at the Artist Roster, and all the new models.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator"/>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Jackson 2021 Artist Models: A Few Changes&#8230;</h2>



<p>There are not a lot of huge surprises in the Jackson 2021 Artist showcase this year. There are a few new models, and a few new colors. Some of the established models have some new specs. But I don&#8217;t blame them at all.</p>



<p>Its been a tough year for everyone.</p>



<p>We are seeing either small releases, or delayed releases from a lot of guitar companies this year. With no Winter NAAM show, it&#8217;s been excusable. This doesn&#8217;t mean that Jackson hasn&#8217;t released some cool stuff. It&#8217;s just limited. </p>



<p>Jackson comes up on our site pretty frequently, and I often recommend the <a href="/best-guitar-for-beginners/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="732">JS22 series</a> to new guitarists who are on a budget. Jackson&#8217;s regular lineup is pretty vast this year, with new colors for most models. We should have an article about those this week, as well.</p>



<p>Despite the odds this year, the Jackson 2021 Artist series has some pretty cool offerings. Some of the features are things Jackson has never done before.</p>



<p>Let&#8217;s take a look at the one that had people scratching their heads first!</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator"/>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Misha Mansoor So-Cal 2PT</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="332" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2904008827_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-1024x332.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1848" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2904008827_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-1024x332.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2904008827_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-600x194.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2904008827_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-300x97.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2904008827_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-768x249.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2904008827_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-1536x498.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2904008827_jac_ins_frt_01_rr.png 1700w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>Misha has made quite the career playing in his band, Periphery. He has several models made by Jackson with his signature on it, but this one is confusing to a lot of people. I can explain&#8230;</p>



<p>Misha is mostly known for playing 6, 7, and <a href="/cheap-8-string-guitar/" data-lasso-id="733">8 string guitars</a> in his Prog Metal projects. But there&#8217;s a little known secret about Misha&#8230;he has a <a href="/the-best-fender-strat-alternative-in-2021/" data-lasso-id="734">Baby Blue Fender Custom Shop Strat</a> that he LOVES.</em> In fact, at one point&#8230;it was his favorite guitar.</p>



<p>There were several parts of <a href="/periphery-djent-is-not-a-genre-album-review/" data-lasso-id="218837">periphery&#8217;s last album</a> HAIL STAN that have this Stratocaster in it! You would have never known Misha was so fond of a Strat, right? </p>



<p>The Jackson 2021 Artist lineup features 3 Misha sigs, and this one is a tribute to his favorite Strat. Jackson is owned by Fender, so they took a lot of liberties with the design.</p>



<p><em>&#8220;The lightweight basswood So-Cal body is paired to a bolt-on caramelized maple neck with rock-solid graphite reinforcement to withstand the rigors of the road and an oiled back finish for that smooth playing feel. The speed neck is topped with a 20”-radius caramelized flame maple fingerboard with comfortable rolled edges, 22 jumbo stainless steel frets and white dot inlays outlined in black. </em></p>



<p><em>Glow-in-the-dark Luminlay® side dots provide exceptional <a href="/how-to-find-fretboard-notes-guitar/" data-lasso-id="735">fretboard guidance</a> on the darkest of stages, while a heel-mount truss rod adjustment wheel allows for quick neck tweaks on the go.</em></p>



<p><em>The Bare Knuckle Ragnarok humbucking bridge and Bare Knuckle Trilogy single-coil middle and neck pickups radiate with aggressive, muscular tone with a slightly compressed vintage twang. This instrument’s versatile voice can be explored with a five-way pickup blade switch and single volume and tone controls.</em></p>



<p><em>A premium Gotoh® Custom 510 tremolo system, Graph Tech® TUSQ® XL nut and Gotoh die-cast locking tuning machines combine for smooth and effortless dynamic tremolo action with outstanding tuning stability.&#8221;</em></p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1000" height="600" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/jksn_2904008827_HIGHLIGHT1.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-1849" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/jksn_2904008827_HIGHLIGHT1.jpg 1000w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/jksn_2904008827_HIGHLIGHT1-600x360.jpg 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/jksn_2904008827_HIGHLIGHT1-300x180.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/jksn_2904008827_HIGHLIGHT1-768x461.jpg 768w" sizes="(max-width: 1000px) 100vw, 1000px" /><figcaption>The neck is GORGEOUS!</figcaption></figure>



<p>This is a great addition to the Jackson 2021 Artist lineup, and I think it offers something different for <a href="/6-epic-guitar-brands-that-arent-gibson-or-fender/" data-lasso-id="44682">fans of Charvel</a> and Fender. This is  <a href="/mike-rutherford-uses-a-200-squier-strat-heres-why/" data-lasso-id="11262">hot-rodded Strat</a> that comes with all premium features. You can see Misha testing last year&#8217;s Custom shop model <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?app=desktop&#038;v=41xTf89tGiU&#038;feature=youtu.be" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="736" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">here.</a> Last year&#8217;s model was a &#8220;relic&#8221; version, and a little more pricey than this model.</p>



<p>Moving on!</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">PRO SERIES SIGNATURE CHRISTIAN ANDREU RHOADS</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="373" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Rhoades-1024x373.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1850" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Rhoades-1024x373.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Rhoades-600x219.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Rhoades-300x109.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Rhoades-768x280.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Rhoades-1536x560.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Rhoades.png 1700w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>The <a href="/ibanez-vs-jackson/" data-lasso-id="166814">Jackson Rhoads is a legendary</a> instrument at this point. We have seen it in so many different incarnations and signatures at this point, and I think we will see it for years to come. Between the Rhoads and The Kelley, we have Jackson&#8217;s biggest contributions to <a href="/3-best-harley-benton-guitars-for-metal-review/" data-lasso-id="189584" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank" data-lasso-name="3 BEST Harley Benton Guitars For Metal: INSANE Deals!">metal guitar</a> styles.</p>



<p>Christian is best known for his role in the French metal band <strong>Gojira.</strong> This isn&#8217;t the first time we are seeing him in the sig series lineup. However, this <a href="/the-best-signature-guitars-in-2021/" data-lasso-id="14304">model is brand new for the Jackson 2021 Artist</a> series, and meant to be an affordable option.</p>



<p>It has many of the Pro Series features that you expect:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>Compound Radius </li><li>Flame Maple Top</li><li>Single Jackson Humbucker</li><li>Black Hardware</li><li>Locking Tuners</li></ul>



<p>If you have always wanted a stripped-down, single Hum Rhoads guitar&#8230; now is the time to get one! These should be coming in at just under $900 for MSRP. This is a great addition to the Jackson 2021 Artist lineup, and totally unexpected.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator"/>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">PRO SERIES SIGNATURE BRANDON ELLIS KELLY</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="390" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Kelley-1024x390.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1851" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Kelley-1024x390.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Kelley-600x229.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Kelley-300x114.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Kelley-768x293.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Kelley-1536x585.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Kelley.png 1700w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>Speaking of unexpected! This Jackson 2021 Artist addition was totally out of left field for me. We have yet another single humbucker guitar, which is a feature that I think more companies should offer. We have seen a few from <a href="/the-2021-esp-lineup-amazing-guitars-to-ring-in-the-new-year/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="737">ESP this year</a>, but it&#8217;s still a &#8220;rare&#8221; feature.</p>



<p>Brandon is the guitarist for metalcore giants, <strong>The Black Dahlia Murder.</strong> he has been a Jackson artist for a few years now, and this year he gets his dream axe:</p>



<p><em>&#8220;This signature Kelly features lightwood and resonant poplar body-wings and a through-body maple neck with graphite reinforcement and scarf joint for enhanced sustain, stability and comfort. Built for pulverizing speed, a 12”-16” compound radius bound ebony fingerboard hosts 24 jumbo frets and reverse pearloid sharkfin inlays.</em></p>



<p><em>Ready to roar, this sig model is also equipped with a <a href="/humbuckers-vs-single-coil-pickups/" data-lasso-id="738">single Seymour Duncan® Parallel Axis bridge humbucking pickup</a>. This humbucker rips with plenty of over-wound attitude for bright, expressive high-end and endless sustain, while a <a href="/floyd-rose-bridge-explained/" data-lasso-id="167825">Floyd</a> Rose® 1000 Series double-locking tremolo system provides exceptional dynamic response and reliable tuning stability throughout extreme pitch changes.</em></p>



<p><em>Other features include a single volume control, <a href="/how-to-hold-an-electric-guitar-properly-guide/" data-lasso-id="739">Dunlop® dual-locking strap buttons</a> and Jackson sealed die-cast tuners.</em></p>



<p><em>As distinctive as its namesake, the <strong>Pro Series Signature Brandon Ellis Kelly™ </strong>comes in a distinct Green Crackle finish with reverse pointed 6-in-line black headstock with green Jackson logo and black hardware.&#8221;</em></p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1000" height="600" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/kelley-2.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-1852" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/kelley-2.jpg 1000w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/kelley-2-600x360.jpg 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/kelley-2-300x180.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/kelley-2-768x461.jpg 768w" sizes="(max-width: 1000px) 100vw, 1000px" /></figure>



<p>Being a fan of all things &#8220;Explorer shaped&#8221;, this is the coolest addition to the Jackson 2021 Artist Lineup to me! It has everything you could want, all killer-no filler. I even like the crackle finishes from the 1980&#8217;s that are making a comeback! I think this would be a cool part of any lineup, not just the Jackson 2021 Artist series.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">PRO SERIES SIGNATURE MARTY FRIEDMAN MF-1</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="327" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty-1024x327.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1853" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty-1024x327.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty-600x192.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty-300x96.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty-768x245.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty-1536x491.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty.png 1700w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>I don&#8217;t think Marty Friedman needs any introduction. He is a legend in the metal world, starting with his early work with Jason Becker in the 1980&#8217;s. Later he became what we know as the &#8216;quintessential&#8221; <strong>Megadeth</strong> Guitarist. he is the person we compare all other Megadeth guitarists to, right?</p>



<p>Marty&#8217;s Jackson 2021 Artist model is not that different from the previous ones. Based on the Monarch shapes, and set up to Marty&#8217;s specs. But the Jackson 2021 Artist model introduces the &#8220;Purple Mirror&#8221; finish. <strong>The Pics do NOT do it justice!</strong></p>



<p>&#8220;<em>This 24.75”-scale signature model is loaded with great features to match the demanding needs of an elite and intricate player like Friedman — but at an affordable price.</em></p>



<p><em>Friedman’s signature MF-1 features a resonant mahogany with a striking cracked mirror top. A mahogany set-neck features a scarf joint and graphite-reinforcement rods to protect against environmental depredations. A 12”-radius bound ebony fingerboard with 22 jumbo frets and pearloid block inlays is an ideal playing surface for swift licks or articulate chord work.</em></p>



<p><em>Marty Friedman dual passive EMG® MF signature humbucking pickups deliver a sound that sings and screams. This axe is also equipped with two volume and two tone controls as well as a three-way pickup toggle switch to wrangle all of the subtle nuances out of your guitar tone. The Jackson TOM-style adjustable bridge with anchored tailpiece and die-cast locking tuners creates improved intonation and enhanced sustain</em>.</p>



<p>Never fear being upstaged with the all-new cracked Purple Mirror finish, complete with 3-ply white/abalone/white body binding, all-black hardware and reverse Jackson 3&#215;3 (3 over, 3 under) AT-1 black headstock.&#8221;</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1000" height="600" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty-2.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-1854" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty-2.jpg 1000w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty-2-600x360.jpg 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty-2-300x180.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/marty-2-768x461.jpg 768w" sizes="(max-width: 1000px) 100vw, 1000px" /></figure>



<p>I wish I could find a picture that shows off this finish better! It&#8217;s really cool, and a throwback to <strong>KISS</strong> and other bands of the 70&#8217;s and 80&#8217;s. This is probably the least &#8220;original&#8221; of the Jackson 2021 Artist lineup. But Marty is not a &#8216;tech&#8221; guy when it comes to gear. he is just a player.</p>



<p>Finally&#8230;we get to the most popular of the Jackson 2021 Artist series&#8230;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Misha Mansoor 6 &amp; 7 String Juggernaut Evertune Models</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="768" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/jackson-new-misha-juggernaut-1024x768.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-1855" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/jackson-new-misha-juggernaut-1024x768.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/jackson-new-misha-juggernaut-600x450.jpg 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/jackson-new-misha-juggernaut-300x225.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/jackson-new-misha-juggernaut-768x576.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/jackson-new-misha-juggernaut.jpg 1400w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>The Jackson 2021 Artist Lineup would not be complete without a <a href="/the-complete-guide-to-7-string-guitar/" data-lasso-id="740">Misha 6 and 7 string guitar</a>! It&#8217;s been several years now, and only subtle changes hve been made to the Juggernaut. Until now!</p>



<p>Now we have <a href="/the-top-5-guitars-evertune-bridge/" data-lasso-id="741">the Evertune bridge installed</a>, along with the usual features&#8230;7 string first:</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="316" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914017527_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-1024x316.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1856" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914017527_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-1024x316.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914017527_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-600x185.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914017527_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-300x92.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914017527_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-768x237.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914017527_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-1536x473.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914017527_jac_ins_frt_01_rr.png 1700w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>&#8220;<em>The <strong>Pro Series Signature Misha Mansoor Juggernaut ET7</strong> offers a 26.5” scale length, basswood body and caramelized bolt-on maple neck with rock-solid graphite reinforcement, wrap-around heel and oiled back finish for greater playing comfort. Enjoy blazing fast leads with a 20” flat radius caramelized maple fingerboard featuring rolled edges, 24 jumbo frets and offset dot inlays. </em></p>



<p><em>Luminous side dots will guide your way even on the darkest of stages, while the heel-mount truss rod adjustment wheel allows for convenient neck relief tweaks</em>.&#8221;</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="326" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914016570_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-1024x326.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1857" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914016570_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-1024x326.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914016570_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-600x191.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914016570_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-300x96.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914016570_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-768x245.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914016570_jac_ins_frt_01_rr-1536x490.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/2914016570_jac_ins_frt_01_rr.png 1700w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p><em>&#8220;The <strong>Pro Series Signature Misha Mansoor Juggernaut ET6 </strong>offers a 25.5” scale length, basswood body and caramelized bolt-on maple neck with graphite reinforcement, wrap-around heel and oiled back finish for greater playing comfort. Enjoy blazing fast leads with a 20” flat radius caramelized maple fingerboard featuring rolled edges, 24 jumbo frets and offset dot inlays. </em></p>



<p><em>Luminous side dots will guide your way even on the darkest of stages, while the heel-mount truss rod adjustment wheel allows for convenient neck relief tweaks.&#8221;</em></p>



<p>Both models sit at about the $1200 price range, and are nearly identical to last year&#8217;s Juggernaut models. But the Jackson 2021 Artist Series adds in the Evertune bridge, which is a game changer for anyone that has used it!</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Jackson 2021 Artist Lineup: Final Thoughts&#8230;</h2>



<p>As I said earlier, it has been a rough year for everyone. Guitarists, and guitar companies alike have seen shortages. Logistics problems. factory delays. It has been the paramount of bad years for a great many industries.</p>



<p>To be fair, I am impressed that so many companies thus far have released so many models despite Covid, and the lack of a physical NAAM show. I have a few more Companies and models to take a look at this next week. I&#8217;m glad the industry has held resilient for the most part.</p>
