<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Evertune Bridge: A Modern Solution For An Ancient Problem</h2>



<p>One of the biggest problems of being a guitar player, is a blessing and a curse: Your Gear. And the biggest problem by far that you constantly hear about guitar, is tuning. This is because even the strongest woods and best hardware parts are <em>fickle</em>. The guitar staying in tune is reliant on several factors:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>The Nut</li><li>Tuning machines</li><li>Setup/Intonation</li><li>Strings</li><li>Temperature/Climate</li><li>Bridge</li></ul>



<p>This is a lot to deal with. You can go over <a href="/anatomy-of-the-electric-guitar-guide/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="1131"><strong>all the parts of a guitar that make the tuning stable</strong></a>, and<em> still</em> have problems at the end of the day. For a person that has guitar as a hobby, this is just a small inconvenience. But if you play shows regularly as a gigging artist, this can be a<strong> huge</strong> problem.</p>



<p>Most guitarists have to setup their guitars when the seasons change, as wood shifts. This is to ensure that the tuning stability is perfect every time you hit the stage. Professional touring guitarists usually have a technician for this job, to keep the guitars on tour in tip-top shape. being a guitar tech on the road is a full time job, that takes a ton of time and patience.</p>



<p>But what if you could bypass having those constant setups and problems? <a href="/what-is-an-evertune-bridge-the-1-guide/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="1132"><strong>We have already talked about how The Evertune bridge system works</strong></a>,  and what an amazing innovation it is. The Evertune is fully mechanical, and has no electronic parts or gimmicks.</p>



<p>Originally, Evertune bridges would have to be retrofit in your guitar, and you would have to<a href="https://www.evertune.com/shop/index.php" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="1133" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"><strong> contact the people at Evertune </strong></a>to have the process done. Over the past few years, many companies have adopted the Evertune Bridge for their most popular models, and today&#8230;we take a look at the top 5 that I have been able to try out!</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">#5 The Framus Stormbender</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="908" height="463" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Framus-Stormbender.jpg" alt="evertune bridge" class="wp-image-2667" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Framus-Stormbender.jpg 908w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Framus-Stormbender-600x306.jpg 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Framus-Stormbender-300x153.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Framus-Stormbender-768x392.jpg 768w" sizes="(max-width: 908px) 100vw, 908px" /></figure>



<p>Framus is known for making high quality, unique designs at a very high level of proficiency. These guitars are <a href="/cort-guitars-a-wonderful-high-quality-brand/" data-lasso-id="5752">usually considered &#8220;boutique&#8221;</a> since they are made in smaller batches, and often they are very expensive. Due to the popularity of this guitar, they have made a more affordable model.</p>



<p>The Framus Stormbender is <strong>Devin Townsend&#8217;s</strong> signature guitar. If you are a fan of avant-garde progressive metal, then you probably know all about The Devin Townsend Project. Devin started his career being the front man for<strong> Steve Vai </strong>way back in 1992. Devin has had many other bands since then and has earned the nickname &#8220;Heavy Devy&#8221;.</p>



<p>Devin was one of the early Evertune bridge users and he has used it since Framus signed him as an artist. Devin often plays 200 shows a year during a tour cycle, and he has said the Evertune Bridge literally changed his life and made touring so much easier. His more affordable signature model has tons of amazing specs:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>Mahogany Body</strong></li><li><strong>Flame maple Top</strong></li><li><a href="/parts-of-an-electric-guitar/" data-lasso-id="246470"><strong>Set Neck Construction</strong></a></li><li><strong>Ebony Fretboard</strong></li><li><strong>25.5 Scale Length</strong></li><li><strong>22 Extra Jumbo Frets</strong></li><li><strong>Graphtech Nut</strong></li><li><strong>Fishman Fluence Pickups</strong></li><li><strong>Graphtech Locking Tuners</strong></li></ul>



<p>This beautiful guitar is Made In Germany and comes with all of the bells and whistles that it&#8217;s $5000 brothers usually have. The only difference is the less complicated finish, and few missing features like the LED lights. But this amazing instrument comes with everything you need!</p>



<p>The Graphtech nut and tuning machines are the best of the best when it comes to keeping your strings in tune. With the case of the Evertune bridge, these features are almost superfluous. But the locking tuners certainly make string changes easier and faster.</p>



<p>The Fishman Fluence pickups are designed by Devin himself, and with the push/pull tone knob configuration you can get just about any sound you want. Not only do you have the two Fishman voices, but you also have a coil split option to get single coil sounds. This gives Devin his huge, versatile sound in the studio and on stage.</p>



<p>Is the Stormbender worth the money? I think it definitely is! This is by far the most expensive option on our list but you have to realize that this is a boutique company. This is a case of &#8220;you get what you pay for&#8221; without a doubt. The workmanship is next level compared to most production guitars and the attention to detail is <em>second to none</em>. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Is The Evertune Bridge Right For You?</h2>



<p>At the end of the day, that&#8217;s the big question isn&#8217;t it? Is the Evertune system something you really need? There are many pros, and a few cons. The main con being that the bridge adds a little bit of weight to your guitar, and some guitarists do not get along with the &#8220;feel&#8221; of the bridge.</p>



<p>Personally, I would have loved to have my guitars outfitted with the Evertune bridge back when I was gigging. <strong><span style="color: #9b51e0;" class="ugb-highlight"><a href="/learning-guitar-guide-what-to-expect/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="1150">I didn&#8217;t have a guitar tech for most of my career</a></span></strong>, so I was left to my own experience when it came to working on my equipment. This was ararely ever ideal since things seem to go wrong at the worst time! </p>



<p>If you are a professional guitarist, I think the Evertune bridge is something that can save you a lot of time and effort at the end of the day. After the initial setup, you never have to worry about going out of tune in the middle of a song again. Likewise, this is a valuable tool when you are in the studio as well. The best way to know if Evertune is right for you, is to try it out!</p>

<div id="faq-question-1617313379033" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">Is Evertune worth it?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>If you are a professional guitarist that plays a ton of shows, or you do a good amount of studio work, then the Evertune bridge could give you a huge advantage. The operation is strictly mechanical, and your guitar never goes out of tune.</p>

</div>

<div id="faq-question-1617313476670" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">Are factory installed Evertune bridges set up out of the box?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>Most companies definitely set the Evertune bridge to standard tuning before leaving the factory. Should you want to change something about it, like string gauge, you will have to adjust it.</p>

</div>
</div>
<div id="faq-question-1617313564648" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">Is Evertune hard to setup?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>The Evertune may seem very complex at first. But the bridge is easily adjusted once you get the hang of it. Once you have chosen a tuning and a string gauge, you never have to worry about going out of tune again.</p>

</div>

<h3 class="rank-math-question ">Do professional guitarists use Evertune?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>Lots of popular artists have starting using the Evertune bridge over the last few years. It makes life on the road much easier, as you never have to &#8220;setup&#8221; your gigging guitars. people like Devin Townsend, Ola Englund, and Ben Weinman have used Evertune for years.</p>
