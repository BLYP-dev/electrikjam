This sleek single-cutaway guitar is a stripped-down beast, borrowing its silhouette from the famed Eclipse series but turning the intensity up a notch with pro-grade features and a no-nonsense attitude.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>ESP LTD Deluxe EC-01FT Specs &amp; Hardware</strong></h2>

<p>Let’s start with the basics. The EC-01FT features a <strong>mahogany body</strong> paired with a <strong>3-piece mahogany neck</strong>, utilizing ESP&#8217;s <strong>set-thru construction</strong> for seamless access to all 22 frets. </p>



<p>The <strong>Macassar ebony fingerboard</strong>—known for its durability and dark, striking aesthetic—comes loaded with <strong>extra-jumbo stainless steel frets</strong>. These frets aren’t just a durability upgrade; they’re designed for smoother bending and enhanced precision during intricate solos.</p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote has-x-small-font-size is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p>With its flat-top body and clean electronics layout, there’s nothing to get in the way of everything from intense riffs and huge sizzling power chords to melodic lead lines. It features a comfortable 24.75” scale with a smooth set-thru heel construction, so there’s nothing to impede you as you grab the highest frets. It provides a classic mahogany body and three-piece mahogany neck to prioritize huge tones, and adds a distinctive brushed matte black pickguard to let you stand out from the crowd. <a href="https://www.espguitars.com/products/32046-ec-01ft" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" data-lasso-id="270951">ESP</a></p>
</blockquote>



<p>The neck itself sports ESP’s <strong>thin “U” profile</strong>, a favorite among players who want comfort for long sessions without compromising speed. Whether you’re shredding solos or delivering palm-muted riffage, this neck design keeps your hand relaxed and your speed blazing.</p>

<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Pickups Are Engineered For Brutality</strong></h3>



<p>Where the EC-01FT truly shines is its focus on tone, driven by the newly developed <strong>Custom 14 humbucker</strong> by Seymour Duncan. </p>



<p>This pickup is a triple ceramic monster, blending DNA from the <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/best-seymour-duncan-pickups-for-metal/" data-type="post" data-id="4588" data-lasso-id="270939">Seymour Duncan Custom and Black Winter pickups</a></strong>. It’s a tone chameleon, capable of moving from sharp, high-gain riffs to pristine cleans.</p>



<p>Here’s why it stands out:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>High Output for Metalheads</strong>: If you’re into genres where “chug” is more a requirement than an option, the Custom 14 delivers the goods. Think articulate lows with just the right amount of saturation to keep things tight.</li>



<li><strong>Coil-Splitting Versatility</strong>: A push-pull tone knob adds even more functionality, splitting the humbucker into single-coil mode for sparkling cleans or jangly rhythm parts. For guitarists who need versatility in live settings, this is a game-changer.</li>



<li><strong>Tonal Range</strong>: The Custom 14 isn’t just about the grind—it also offers clarity for complex chord voicings and leads.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Bridge &amp; Locking Tuner Updates</strong></h2>



<p>ESP has kitted out the EC-01FT with <strong>TonePros TOM bridge</strong> hardware. This recessed, string-thru design ensures top-tier sustain while keeping the string height low for comfort. </p>



<p>Coupled with <strong>LTD locking tuners</strong>, the guitar stays rock-solid in tune, no matter how much you abuse the whammy bar or push it through heavy riffing.</p>
