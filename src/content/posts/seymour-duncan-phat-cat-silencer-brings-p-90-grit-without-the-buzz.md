<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-dots"/>



<p>In a move that&#8217;s sure to excite tone-chasers and noise-haters alike, Seymour Duncan has just dropped their latest creation: the Phat Cat Silencer. </p>



<p>This humbucker-sized pickup promises to deliver all the growl and grit of a classic P-90 without the infamous 60-cycle hum that&#8217;s been the bane of rock guitarists for decades.</p>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="the-phat-cat-s-meow-features-and-design"><strong>The Phat Cat&#8217;s Meow: Features and Design</strong></h2>



<p>The Phat Cat Silencer isn&#8217;t just about quieting the noise; it&#8217;s about unleashing the full potential of that thick, <strong><a href="/p90-pickups-guide/" data-type="post" data-id="8654" data-lasso-id="266592">edgy P-90 tone</a></strong> in a package that&#8217;s as versatile as it is powerful. </p>



<p>Available in both Vintage and Hot voicings, these pickups are designed to cater to players across the spectrum, from blues purists to high-gain enthusiasts. What sets the Phat Cat Silencer apart is its plug-and-play simplicity. </p>



<p>Designed to fit any guitar routed for humbuckers, it&#8217;s an easy mod that can transform the sound of your axe without requiring any woodwork. </p>



<p>Whether you&#8217;re rocking a Les Paul, an SG, or any other humbucker-equipped guitar, the Phat Cat Silencer promises to slide right in and deliver that coveted P-90 punch.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="tone-talk-what-to-expect"><strong>Tone Talk: What to Expect</strong></h3>



<figure class="wp-block-image alignwide size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="686" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/seymour-duncan-Hot-Phat-Cat-Silencer-for-les-paul-1024x686.png" alt="" class="wp-image-15517" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/seymour-duncan-Hot-Phat-Cat-Silencer-for-les-paul-1024x686.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/seymour-duncan-Hot-Phat-Cat-Silencer-for-les-paul-600x402.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/seymour-duncan-Hot-Phat-Cat-Silencer-for-les-paul-300x201.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/seymour-duncan-Hot-Phat-Cat-Silencer-for-les-paul-768x514.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/seymour-duncan-Hot-Phat-Cat-Silencer-for-les-paul.png 1156w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>The ceramic magnet design provides a slightly scooped midrange with pronounced treble, making these pickups perfect for cutting through a mix. Add a fuzz pedal and a cranked tube amp, and you&#8217;ve got a recipe for pure rock &#8216;n&#8217; roll nirvana. </p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="has-small-font-size"><strong>&#8220;They deliver a true P90 tone without the buzz, perfect for those looking to explore the feel and response of a P90 without routing out a guitar,&#8221; says Seymour Duncan. It&#8217;s this combination of classic tone and modern noiseless technology that makes the Phat Cat Silencer a game-changer for gigging musicians and studio cats alike.</strong></p>
</blockquote>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="pricing-and-value-proposition"><strong>Pricing and Value Proposition</strong></h3>



<p><strong><a href="/recommends/seymour-duncan-hot-phat-cat-silencer/" data-lasso-id="266593">Priced at $149 for the gold-covered version</a></strong> and $139 for nickel, with set options available, the Phat Cat Silencer is positioned as a premium upgrade that won&#8217;t break the bank. </p>



<p></div>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-s-it-for-"><strong>Who&#8217;s It For?</strong></h3>



<p>For players who&#8217;ve always loved the idea of P-90s but shied away due to noise concerns, the Phat Cat Silencer offers a fairly unique solution. It&#8217;s equally suited for the blues-rock veteran looking to tame their rig for studio work or the punk rocker who wants to push their amp to the edge without drowning in noise.</p>



<p>Me? I think P90 pickups sound great in any context, even metal. Don&#8217;t believe me? Just ask <strong><a href="/how-to-sound-like-mastodon/" data-type="post" data-id="10767" data-lasso-id="266594">Brent Hinds from Mastodon</a></strong> – he loves his P90 pickups. They&#8217;re part of the reason he gets such an organic, warm sound on his lead and rhythm playing. </p>
