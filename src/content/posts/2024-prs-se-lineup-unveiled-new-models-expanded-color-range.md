<h2 class="wp-block-heading">KEY TAKEAWAYS</h2>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Introduction of New Models and Colors:</strong> PRS is launching its 2024 season with fresh models and colorways for its SE line-up, which has been highly popular for its affordability and quality.</li>



<li><strong>Elevated Line-Up:</strong> PRS labels the new SE line-up as “elevated”, building on the existing SE line and including the first-ever Custom 24 model in this series.</li>



<li><strong>Comments from PRS&#8217; COO:</strong> Jack Higginbotham, the COO at PRS Guitars, emphasizes the SE Series&#8217; role in catering to both beginners and professionals. He indicates that the new models are inspired by the company’s history, innovations, and the team&#8217;s experiences as players and builders.</li>
</ul>



<p></div>



<p>PRS is kicking 2024 off with a bang, adding in new models and new colorways to its insanely popular SE line-up of <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/does-the-do-it-all-guitar-exist/" data-type="post" data-id="5415" data-lasso-id="251220">affordable (and thoroughly excellent) guitars</a></strong>. </p>



<p>PRS describes the new line-up as “elevated”, building on the foundations set by its OG SE line and adding in its first Custom 24 model to the series.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Here’s the skinny from PRS&#8217; COO, Jack Higginbotham: </p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p><strong>“We have so much inspiration and energy behind product development for the SE Series because it is a range of instruments that we know is supporting everyone from beginning players to professional musicians,” says Jack Higginbotham, COO at PRS Guitars.</strong></p>



<p><strong>“We draw from our Maryland innovations, our history, and our own curiosities as players and builders. I think these new models will continue the SE Series promise to deliver quality tools for musicians.”</strong></p>
</blockquote>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="PRS_SE_CE_">PRS SE CE 24</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">
<iframe title="The SE CE 24 | Demo | PRS Guitars" width="500" height="281" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/nlDJcM7M0x8?feature=oembed" frameborder="0" allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture; web-share" referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin" allowfullscreen></iframe>

<p>The SE CE 24 model is PRS’s attempt at blending traditional elements with modern design. </p>



<p>This model marks the CE model&#8217;s entry into the SE Series, combining the classic Custom 24 structure—a maple top, mahogany back, and rosewood fretboard—with a 25” scale length bolt-on maple neck. </p>



<p>It&#8217;s equipped with PRS 85/15 “S” humbuckers and a patented PRS tremolo bridge. A new ergonomic heel design aims to improve playability, especially in the higher frets.</p>



<p>Color options include <strong>Black Cherry, Blood Orange, Turquoise, and Vintage Sunburst.</strong></p>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="PRS_SE_CE__Highlights:">PRS SE CE 24 Highlights:</h3>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Blend of traditional and modern elements.</li>



<li>Introduction of the CE model to the SE Series.</li>



<li>Features include a maple top, mahogany back, rosewood fretboard, a 25” scale length bolt-on maple neck, PRS 85/15 “S” humbuckers, and a patented PRS tremolo bridge.</li>



<li>New ergonomic heel design for enhanced playability.</li>



<li>Available colors: Black Cherry, Blood Orange, Turquoise, and Vintage Sunburst.</li>
</ul>
