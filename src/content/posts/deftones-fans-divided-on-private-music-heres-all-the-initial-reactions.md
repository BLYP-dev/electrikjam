<p>The long-awaited <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/album-reviews/deftones-private-music-review/" data-type="album-reviews" data-id="17965">Private Music album from Deftones</a></strong> has landed, and as expected, the internet is buzzing — or more accurately, <strong>Reddit is conflicted</strong>. </p>



<p>While the record has sparked excitement among longtime fans, many are asking the same question: is it just more of the same?</p>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Is Deftones&#8217; New Private Music Any Good? Here&#8217;s How Fans Are Reacting&#8230;</strong></h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img decoding="async" width="894" height="655" src="/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/71dBHM6iLrL._UF8941000_QL80_.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-17967" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/71dBHM6iLrL._UF8941000_QL80_.jpg 894w, /wp-content/uploads/2025/08/71dBHM6iLrL._UF8941000_QL80_-600x440.jpg 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2025/08/71dBHM6iLrL._UF8941000_QL80_-300x220.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2025/08/71dBHM6iLrL._UF8941000_QL80_-768x563.jpg 768w" sizes="(max-width: 894px) 100vw, 894px" /></figure>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Familiar Sound, Divided Opinions</h3>



<p>If there&#8217;s one thing fans can agree on, it&#8217;s that <em>Private Music</em> sounds&#8230; like Deftones. That’s not a dig but for some listeners, that signature dreamy heaviness and atmospheric layering feels a bit too predictable.</p>



<p>One top comment summed it up perfectly:</p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="has-base-font-size">“It’s good, but it sounds like everything Deftones have ever done. So in that respect it’s kind of boring, but I appreciate the consistency.”</p>
</blockquote>



<p>Across Reddit threads and fan forums, the reaction has been mostly positive — but it’s clear that <em>Private Music</em> hasn’t blown everyone away. Some listeners love the album’s familiar textures, while others hoped for a few more surprises.</p>



<p></div>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Fan Favorites Emerging Early</h3>



<p>Despite the mixed reactions, certain tracks are rising to the top. <strong>“Infinite Source”</strong> has been mentioned over and over again as a highlight — although not without controversy.</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>“Infinite Source hit me like a ton of bricks. Definitely a top 10 song for me.”</li>



<li>“Infinite Source might be one of the corniest sounding songs of theirs I’ve ever heard.”</li>
</ul>



<p>Meanwhile, <strong>“Milk of the Madonna”</strong> and <strong>“Cut Hands”</strong> are also being singled out as standout tracks, with many praising Steph’s riff work and Abe’s always-tight drumming.</p>



<p></div>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Is It Better Than Gore and Ohms?</h3>



<p>That’s the other big debate. Plenty of fans are saying <em>Private Music</em> is a <em>step up</em> from the band’s last two albums, <em>Gore</em> and <em>Ohms</em>:</p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="has-small-font-size">“Big step up from Gore and Ohms, feels like a real successor to what came before it.”</p>
</blockquote>



<p>But not everyone agrees. A few loyalists are defending <em>Ohms</em> as one of the band’s strongest releases in recent years — and some think <em>Private Music</em> just doesn’t stack up.</p>



<p>The average fan rating? Reddit’s unofficial score seems to float around a <strong>6 or 7 out of 10</strong>. Solid, but not a mind-blower.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">A Soundtrack for Deftones Lifers</h3>



<p>In the end, the album might be best appreciated by the fans who’ve been on the journey since the <em>Adrenaline</em> and <em>White Pony</em> days. Newcomers? They may want to start elsewhere.</p>



<p>One user said it best:</p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p class="has-base-font-size">“If you love Deftones, you’ll love this album. But it’s not likely to convert anyone who doesn’t already care.”</p>
</blockquote>



<p>That said, there&#8217;s also a sense of respect. For a band that&#8217;s more than 30 years into their career, staying this consistent <em>is</em> impressive — even if the surprises are fewer these days.</p>
