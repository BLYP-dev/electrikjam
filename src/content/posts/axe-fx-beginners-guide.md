<p><strong>Steve Vai, Metallica, John Petrucci, John Mayer. They all use Axe FX. But what is it and how does it work? Here&#8217;s everything you need to know…</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-dots"/>


<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-dots"/>



<p>First released in 2006 by Fractal Audio Systems, the Axe-Fx is a high-end digital guitar processor that has become a go-to tool for serious guitar players, both professional and amateur. </p>



<p>But what exactly does it do, and why has it caught on so widely? This guide will explain everything you need to know about Axe-Fx, what makes it so good, and why bands like Metallica are making the switch from analog to digital amp modellers. </p>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="What_is_the_Axe_FX?"><strong>What is Axe FX?</strong></h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/what-is-axe-fx-1024x683.png" alt="" class="wp-image-14264" title="" srcset="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/what-is-axe-fx-1024x683.png 1024w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/what-is-axe-fx-600x400.png 600w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/what-is-axe-fx-300x200.png 300w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/what-is-axe-fx-768x512.png 768w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/what-is-axe-fx.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>The Axe-Fx is best described as a multi-effects unit and amp modeler. It gives guitarists access to a wide variety of tones and effects all within one unit, eliminating the need to connect various pedals. </p>



<p>Models feature powerful DSP to run ultra-realistic amp simulations and capture the nuances of classic effects.</p>



<p>If you’re a touring musician, lugging gear around –&nbsp;things like amps and pedals and cabinets –&nbsp;isn’t just a pain in the ass, it costs you extra money too. </p>



<p>On tour, weight matters. This is why more and more <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/guitarists-that-use-axe-fx/" data-type="post" data-id="7103" data-lasso-id="260849">artists have switched to Axe-FX.&nbsp;</a></strong></p>



<p>Imagine the scene: you’re booked to play a string of shows along the West Coast, culminating in a show in LA. You’re at the penultimate gig and your tube amp blows its top.&nbsp;</p>



<p>You don’t have time to get it fixed, so you’ll need to either borrow one from a friend or –&nbsp;god forbid –&nbsp;break out the plastic and buy a new one.&nbsp;</p>



<p>This is an old-world problem. With something like Axe-FX, this just isn’t an issue: you can plug it direct into the venue’s PA, pair it with a cab, whatever which means you can play the show. </p>



<p>All of your tones, FX, and settings are hermetically and digitally sealed inside the unit itself. And they seldom break (unlike tube amps).&nbsp;</p>



<p>This makes the Axe-Fx incredibly versatile for practice, recording, and live performance applications. But the main USP of using a modeller over a traditional amp stack is reliability whilst on tour. </p>



<p>And increased reliability means fewer surprise costs and a more consistent, predictable sound for each show. </p>

<p>As always, there’s a bunch of different Axe-Fx models available to buy, ranging from older models to newer models and, of course, the most expensive and flagship models.&nbsp;</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">

<li><strong>Axe-Fx</strong> <strong>FM9:</strong>&nbsp;Released in 2021,&nbsp;it&#8217;s a floor-based unit with nine footswitches and four DSP cores,&nbsp;offering most of the Axe-Fx III&#8217;s capabilities in a more compact and portable format.</li>

<ul class="wp-block-list">

<p>You can pick up older models for quite a bit less, though, so if you&#8217;re looking to try Axe-Fx and you don&#8217;t want to spend mega bucks (they ain&#8217;t cheap, obvs), then a used, older model from a reseller could be the way to go. </p>

<p>Each Axe-Fx model is different but they all share some similarities when it comes to setting them up, dialing in tones and FX, and making your way around its user interface (UX).&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="Getting_Started:"><strong>Getting Started</strong></h3>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Connect everything:</strong> Power up your Axe-Fx, plug in your guitar, speaker, and any audio interface or DAW connection. Refer to the manual for specific input/output configurations.</li>



<li><strong>Explore the UI:</strong> The touchscreen interface is your command center. Swipe, tap, and hold to access different functions. The main screen displays the currently selected preset, with amp, effects, and routing blocks visible.</li>



<li><strong>Navigate blocks:</strong> Each block represents a specific amp, effect, or routing function. Tap a block to access its detailed editing menu.</li>



<li><strong>Tweak your sound</strong>: Each block&#8217;s menu offers various parameters for tone sculpting. Explore gain, EQ, effects settings, and more. Don&#8217;t be afraid to experiment!</li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="Building_Your_Tones:"><strong>Building Your Tones</strong></h3>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Preset exploration</strong>: Axe-Fx comes preloaded with diverse presets. Browse them by genre, amp type, or effect focus to find starting points.</li>



<li><strong>Preset editing</strong>: Customize existing presets to your liking. Tap and hold specific parameters to make changes, then save your tweaks.</li>



<li><strong>Building from scratch</strong>: Start with an empty preset and choose your desired amp model. Add effects, adjust routing, and tweak parameters to craft your unique sound.</li>



<li><strong>Global settings</strong>: Access global parameters like master volume, tuner, and MIDI settings through dedicated menus.</li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="Software_and_Updates:"><strong>Software and Updates</strong></h3>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Fractal Audio Axe-Edit</strong>: This free software (more on this below) lets you manage presets, update firmware, and access deeper editing options on your computer.</li>



<li><strong>Firmware updates</strong>: Fractal Audio regularly pumps out firmware updates too, bringing improvements, bug fixes, and new capabilities. </li>
</ul>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="Fractal_Audio_Axe-Edit"><strong>Fractal Audio Axe-Edit</strong></h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="770" src="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/getting-started-with-axe-fx-1024x770.png" alt="" class="wp-image-14262" title="" srcset="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/getting-started-with-axe-fx-1024x770.png 1024w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/getting-started-with-axe-fx-600x451.png 600w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/getting-started-with-axe-fx-300x226.png 300w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/getting-started-with-axe-fx-768x578.png 768w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/getting-started-with-axe-fx.png 1292w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="User_Experience"><strong>User Experience</strong></h4>



<p>Axe-Edit III is a software editor designed for the Axe-Fx III, enhancing the user experience by leveraging the capabilities of a computer interface. </p>



<p>It allows users to utilize a larger display area and input devices such as a full-size keyboard and mouse or touchpad. </p>



<p>This setup significantly simplifies the process of creating and adjusting sounds, making the navigation and manipulation of intricate setups more straightforward and user-friendly.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="Editing_Capabilities"><strong>Editing Capabilities</strong></h4>



<p>The software extends considerable creative control to the user, enabling a comprehensive range of editing actions. </p>



<p>Users can effortlessly manage presets, including viewing, editing, copying, pasting, saving, and swapping. </p>



<p>Beyond presets, Axe-Edit III facilitates the manipulation of scenes, blocks, channels, and user-created cabinet simulations. </p>



<p>The addition of drag-and-drop functionality enhances the efficiency of the workflow, making the editor both powerful and intuitive.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="Workflow_Efficiency"><strong>Workflow Efficiency</strong></h4>



<p>Axe-Edit III is designed to streamline the sound creation process. It includes preset Templates, which serve as starting points to accelerate the development of new tones. </p>



<p>The Block Library, another key feature, allows users to quickly access and utilize their preferred sounds. </p>



<p>These sounds are stored on the user&#8217;s computer, enabling swift retrieval with a single click, thus optimizing the workflow for speed and convenience.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="Beyond_Amps_amp;_Effects_"><strong>Beyond Amps &amp; Effects</strong> </h2>



<p>The Axe-Fx III&#8217;s countless options extend beyond its formidable amp and effects collection. </p>



<p>It also boasts a handy built-in tuner, looper to record/layer guitar tracks, and USB audio interface capabilities. </p>



<p>By connecting to a DAW, guitarists can access best-in-class tone when recording while applying effects in real-time via plugin control software like Axe-Edit. </p>



<p>The unit also features MIDI integration to have parameters controlled by an external MIDI device such as a foot controller.</p>



<p>These extras add further flexibility for both live and studio use. With its comprehensive toolset, the Axe-Fx aims to be an all-encompassing solution. </p>



<p>Guitarists can sculpt world-class tone for virtually any context while controlling every parameter imaginable right from their panel or computer.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="Is_It_Easy_To_Use?_"><strong>Is It Easy To Use? </strong></h2>



<p>With immense capability comes complexity. Some guitarists feel overwhelmed by the Axe-Fx&#8217;s endless options and parameters. </p>



<p>It took me months to really get my head around using it properly; it&#8217;s not because it is hard to use, per se, but because there are just so many potential options and customizations. </p>



<p>The best approach, at least, for me, is to go in with specific goals in mind: I want this sound, or that sound, or this kind of sound with that effect. Narrowing your focus helps to NOT get overwhelmed by all the possibilities which inside Axe-Fx are near-endless. </p>



<p></div>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="Price_amp;_Value"><strong>Price &amp; Value</strong></h2>



<p>Axe-Fx pricing spans a wide gamut depending on the model. Flagship Axe-Fx III units cost nearly $3000, while the oldest Axe-Fx II models run under $500 used. </p>



<p>This high-end gear represents a serious investment. </p>



<p>The expense may only make sense for professionals and dedicated hobbyists. </p>



<p>However, when considering the cost of building a rig with equivalent analog gear, the Axe-Fx may offer more value long-term. Compared spec for spec, this all-in-one tone toolbox certainly delivers despite the initial price tag.</p>
