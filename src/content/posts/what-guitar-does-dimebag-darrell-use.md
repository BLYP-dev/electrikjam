<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="What_Guitar_Did_Dimebag_Darrell_Use?">What Guitar Did Dimebag Darrell Use?</h2>



<div style="color:#32373c;background-color:#ff3399" class="wp-block-genesis-blocks-gb-notice gb-font-size-18 gb-block-notice" data-id="5ca66c"><div class="gb-notice-title" style="color:#fff"><p>The Answer</p></div><div class="gb-notice-text" style="border-color:#ff3399">
<p><strong>Dimebag Darrell was the influential guitarist of Pantera and Damageplan, known for his lightning fast solos and heavy, grooving riffs. Darrell&#8217;s guitar of choice was the Dean Razorback.</strong></p>



<p><strong>His main guitar was a red Dean Razorback fitted with a Floyd Rose tremolo bridge, Seymour Duncan pickups, and his trademark &#8220;Dimebag&#8221; graphics. </strong></p>



<p><strong>This razor shape suited Darrell&#8217;s aggressive thrash and groove metal style. The pointed shape allowed easy access to the upper frets for solos and squeals.</strong></p>



<p><strong>Other notable Dean guitars Dimebag used include ML models, V&#8217;s, and Z models. </strong></p>



<p><strong>However, the Dean Razorback remained his go-to both live and in the studio through his entire career. Dean even released several signature &#8220;Dimebag Razorback&#8221; models built to Darrell&#8217;s specs.</strong></p>

<p></div>



<p>Dimebag Darrell, a name synonymous with revolutionary guitar playing, began his journey with a humble Hofner Les Paul copy. This guitar, a gift on his 11th birthday, was the first step in his path to becoming a guitar legend.</p>



<p>By age 15, Darrell transitioned to his dream guitar, the Dean ML. In an almost serendipitous twist of fate, he received not one, but two Dean ML guitars on the same day. </p>



<p>One was a gift, and the other, a testament to his budding talent, was a prize won in a contest. Among these, the &#8220;Dean from Hell&#8221; stands out. After a brief separation from Darrell, this guitar became his signature instrument, etching its name in the annals of rock history.</p>



<p>In 1994, Dimebag expanded his guitar arsenal significantly. This expansion came as a result of a deal with Washburn, leading to the creation of unique designs like the &#8220;Rebel&#8221; and &#8220;Snakeskin&#8221; guitars. </p>



<p>These designs not only showcased Dimebag&#8217;s personal style but also his desire to push the boundaries of conventional guitar design.</p>



<p>When it comes to amplifiers, Dimebag is best known for his use of the Randall R100ES, particularly on the iconic album &#8220;Cowboys from Hell.&#8221; </p>



<p>His signature sound later evolved with the development of his own <strong>Randall Warhead amp</strong>, a model that became as synonymous with Dimebag as his ferocious playing style.</p>



<p>Dimebag&#8217;s gear list wasn&#8217;t complete without a mention of his guitar effects. Among them, <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/dimebag-darrell-loved-these-guitar-pedals-heres-why/" data-type="post" data-id="10630" data-lasso-id="255056">the Wah and Digitech WH-1 Whammy pedals</a></strong> were significant in shaping his unique sound. </p>



<p>These effects added a new dimension to his playing, allowing him to explore sonic landscapes that were previously uncharted in heavy metal music.</p>



<p>As his career progressed, Dimebag&#8217;s gear list continued to evolve. This evolution was not just a testament to his growth as a musician but also a reflection of his never-ending quest to refine and redefine his sound. </p>



<p>Each piece of gear, be it a guitar, an amp, or an effect pedal, contributed uniquely to his legendary tone and style, leaving an indelible mark on the world of music.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="What_Makes_The_Dean_Razorback_So_Special?_">What Makes The Dean Razorback So Special? </h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Dean-Dimebag-Razorback--1024x683.png" alt="Dean Dimebag Razorback" class="wp-image-13339" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Dean-Dimebag-Razorback--1024x683.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Dean-Dimebag-Razorback--600x400.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Dean-Dimebag-Razorback--300x200.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Dean-Dimebag-Razorback--768x512.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Dean-Dimebag-Razorback-.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>The main reason that the Dean Razorback is so special is that it was designed by <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/famous-guitarists-the-guitars-they-use-a-big-ol-list/" data-type="post" data-id="4292" data-lasso-id="254809">Dimebag Darrell</a></strong>. He worked on and helped workout every aspect of its shape, electronics, and components. Everything was built to his exact requirements. </p>



<p>If you&#8217;re a fan of <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/pantera-albums-ranked-in-order-of-heaviness/" data-type="post" data-id="3542" data-lasso-id="254810">Pantera</a></strong> or want to create something similar to Dimebag&#8217;s legendary tone, the Dean Razorback is the quickest shortcut for doing so. You&#8217;ll also need a killer amp setup and some pedals <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/dimebag-darrell-loved-these-guitar-pedals-heres-why/" data-type="post" data-id="10630" data-lasso-id="254811">(here&#8217;s the pedals used by Dimebag himself). </a></strong></p>



<p>But a solid guitar, built to Dime specifications, is a great place to start if you&#8217;re after that late-1990s Pantera-style guitar tone. </p>



<p>The Dean Razorback comes with a bold V-shaped contour, aggressive pointy edges, and distinctive split headstock. The pickups? It comes equipped with a Seymour Duncan SH-13 Dimebucker in the bridge and a Seymour Duncan 59 in the neck. </p>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<p>Here&#8217;s what puts the Dean Razorback in a league of its own:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Striking Aesthetics:</strong> The Razorback boasts an audacious design. Its V-shape and pointed edges are eye-catching, while the split headstock adds an extra edge.</li>



<li><strong>Dynamic Sound Range:</strong> Known for its versatility, the Razorback delivers everything from heavy metal power chords to high-flying solos with a bright, crisp tone and robust sustain.</li>



<li><strong>Dimebag Darrell&#8217;s Legacy:</strong> As the brainchild of the guitar virtuoso, the Razorback is highly coveted.  Dime put is his heart and soul into this guitar which makes it quite unlike anything else on the market. </li>
</ul>



<p></div>



<p>Of course, the elephant in the room here is that the Dean Razorback is perhaps one of the <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/the-bc-rich-2021-lineup-is-here-new-axes/" data-type="post" data-id="2154" data-lasso-id="254812">ugliest guitars</a></strong> you&#8217;ll ever see. </p>



<p>Beauty resides in the eye of the beholder –&nbsp;and this is even more true when it comes to guitar styles and design – so, please, do not take this as a slight on Darrell, I love the guy and his music, but this guitar? Woof! </p>



<p>While the Dean Razorback ain&#8217;t pretty, it can certainly rip it up with the best of them when it comes to blistering lead and heavy, chuggy rhythm guitar. </p>
