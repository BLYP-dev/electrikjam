<p><strong>8 string guitars can get expensive quickly, but if you’re looking for a cheaper option, you should definitely be looking at this bad boy by Jackson&nbsp;–&nbsp;it is one of the best cheap 8 string guitars you can buy right now…</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator"/>



<p>You want to start playing djent but you do not have the budget <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/the-top-5-extended-range-guitars-for-2021-going-low/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="1047">to drop $1000+ on an 8 string guitar</a></strong>. This is a problem. But as with most things in life, there is a solution.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Cheap 8 String Guitars </strong></h2>



<p>And that solution is to go with a cheap 8 <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/guitar-string-gauges-guide/" data-lasso-id="218958">string guitar</a> over a super-expensive one. If you’re a beginner or someone that just plays for fun, you don’t need a <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/are-expensive-guitar-cables-worth-it-top-5-cables/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="1048">studio-grade 8 string guitar</a></strong> for playing at home and/or jamming with your buddies.</p>



<p>You just need something that’ll get the job done, sound good, pack in some decent pickups, and that plays nicely. And the 8-string guitars listed below all fit the bill. We have included an affordable option, a mid-range option, and a premium option (which is <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/meshuggah-immutable-review/" data-lasso-id="101294">the Meshuggah Signature model)</a>. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Full Specifications</strong></h2>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>One-piece bolted neck: Maple, with graphite reinforcement and scarf joint</strong></li><li><strong>Fretboard: Amaranth</strong></li><li><strong>With fretboard binding</strong></li><li><strong>Piranha fretboard inlays</strong></li><li><strong>Fretboard radius: 12&#8243;-16&#8243;</strong></li><li><strong>24 Jumbo frets</strong></li><li><strong>Scale: 673 mm</strong></li><li><strong>Pickups: 2 High-output Jackson humbucker</strong></li><li><strong>3-Way toggle-switch</strong></li><li><strong>1 Volume and 1 tone control</strong></li><li><strong>Jackson HT8 bridge</strong></li><li><strong>Black hardware</strong></li><li><strong>Colour: Satin black</strong></li></ul>



<p>Having played this guitar, albeit very briefly, and having no idea it was so cheap, I was kind of blown away <strong><a class="rank-math-link" href="https://www.electrikjam.com/recommends/jackson-js32-8-dinky-dka-sbk-ah/" data-lasso-id="1050">when I found out that it cost less than $400.</a></strong></p>



<p>The Jackson pickups – you get 2 High-output Jackson humbuckers were brilliant, giving clear, deep tones even on the lower end of the spectrum which is key <strong><a class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="1051" href="https://www.electrikjam.com/what-guitars-do-meshuggah-use/">when you’re dealing with 7 and 8 string guitars</a></strong>.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Best 8 String Guitar Under $400&nbsp;</strong></h2>



<p>The body and design, in my opinion, are totally gorgeous –&nbsp;it looks like a million bucks up close and in pictures. The Jackson <a data-lasso-id="1052" href="https://www.electrikjam.com/types-of-guitars-guide/">JS32-8 Dinky DKA is also relatively lightweight for an 8 string guitar</a>, coming in at just over 14 pounds.</p>



<p>It also comes in white which, if you like white guitars, is definitely a plus –&nbsp;I think it looks dope. Although I think, given a choice, I would almost certainly go with the black model, just because it will look good for longer –&nbsp;white shows every bit of grime as well as scuffs and bumps.</p>



<p>Another cool thing about this guitar? Its Amaranth fretboard is nice and fast; it’s also pretty thin too, so you can move around pretty easily. Again, it’s not going to rival the more expensive 8-string guitars on the market in this context, but <strong><a class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="1053" href="https://www.electrikjam.com/recommends/jackson-js32-8-dinky-dka-sbk-ah/">for less than $400</a></strong> you really cannot fault this guitar.</p>



<p>If you’re looking for a cheap 8 <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/which-guitar-strings-last-longest/" data-lasso-id="15981">string guitar</a> that looks great and plays like an angel then the Jackson JS32-8 Dinky DKA SBK AH is 100% worth a look. I cannot fathom how Jackson can retail a guitar of this quality for such a small amount of money, but I’ll take it!</p>



<p>Kudos, Jackson! You just earned yourself a lifelong fan.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Verdict: What&#8217;s The Best Cheap 8 String Guitar? </strong></h2>



<p>The entire point of this post is to show you that it isn’t necessary to drop over $1000 on an 8-string guitar. You can still get a killer setup without breaking the bank. I do really like the Jackson JS32 Dinky <strong>but the damn thing is sold-out everywhere.</strong></p>



<p>For this reason, my next choice would be the Ibanez RG8; it is as good as the Jackson JS32-8 Dinky with respect to build quality, tone, and pickups. <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/ibanez-rg8-8-string-electric-guitar/" data-type="lasso-urls" data-id="5409" data-lasso-id="14489" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank" data-lasso-name="Ibanez RG8 8-String Electric Guitar">And it costs exactly the same –&nbsp;$399.99.</a></strong> As affordable options go, the Ibanez RG8 would be our #1 recommendation right now. It is an incredible guitar for not much money at all.</p>



<p>If you want to take things a step further, but still not break the bank, the <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/schecter-nick-johnston-hss-review/" data-lasso-id="40160">Schecter</a> Demon-8 is well worth a look. This guitar is slightly more expensive than the RG8 at $539 but you will notice the difference in quality when you pick it up and when you hear how good its pickups sound. If you can stretch, <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/schecter-demon-8-8-string-electric-guitar-link/" data-type="lasso-urls" data-id="5413" data-lasso-id="14490" rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" target="_blank" data-lasso-name="Schecter Demon-8">get the Schecter Demon-8</a></strong> –&nbsp;it is ridiculously good for how much it costs.</p>

<p>How good is the Schecter Demon-8? Personally, I’d take a Schecter Demon-8 over the more expensive <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/ibanez-guitars/" data-lasso-id="255023">Ibanez M80M Meshuggah Signature model</a>. Why? I prefer the way the Schecter Demon-8 looks. I also prefer its pickups. The Schecter Diamond Active HB-1055 humbuckers are <strong><em>hot-as-f*ck</em></strong> and sound incredible.</p>



<p>What about the Jackson? Well, I just realized that the Jackson JS32-8 Dinky DKA SBK AH is pretty much sold out everywhere. I cannot say I’m surprised; at this price point, it was always going to be <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/best-selling-guitars/" data-lasso-id="19199">extremely popular</a>.</p>



<p>You can still get one, <strong>but you’ll be waiting 6-8 weeks for delivery</strong>. </p>



<p>If you cannot wait, however, the Jackson JS32-8 Dinky DKA SBK AH isn’t the only game in town when it comes to killer (and affordable) 8 <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/schecter-demon-7-review/" data-lasso-id="22585">string guitars</a>. <p>Is it as good as the Jackson JS32-8 Dinky DKA SBK AH? No, not even close. But it is an 8 string guitar with OK pickups that plays great. If you want mega value for money, and you can live with cheaper hardware/components, this extremely cheap 8 string guitar could be worth a punt.</p>
