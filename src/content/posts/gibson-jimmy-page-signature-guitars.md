<p><strong>Gibson Announces Signature Guitar Partnership with Led Zeppelin&#8217;s Jimmy Page</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-dots"/>



<div class="wp-block-ultimate-post-wrapper ultp-block-e263d4"><div class="ultp-wrapper-block">
<p><strong>TL;DR: Gibson has partnered with Led Zeppelin guitarist Jimmy Page on a signature line of guitars, starting with a meticulously recreated version of his iconic double neck model.</strong></p>
</div></div>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-dots"/>



<p>Gibson has announced a new partnership with Led Zeppelin guitarist Jimmy Page that will see the development of a series of signature models bearing Page&#8217;s name.</p>



<p>So if you&#8217;re bank account wasn&#8217;t completely scorched by <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/adam-jones-gibson-les-paul-custom/" data-type="post" data-id="14080" data-lasso-id="260927">Adam Jones&#8217; 1979 Gibson Les Paul</a></strong> and you&#8217;re a fan of Page, get your wallet (and second mortgage) ready because the first model coming is a real doozy. </p>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>And The First is Jimmy Page&#8217;s 1971 Gibson EDS-1275 Double Neck Guitar</strong></h2>



<p>The first release under the partnership will be a Custom Shop recreation of Page&#8217;s famous 1971 Gibson EDS-1275 double neck guitar, which he used extensively with Led Zeppelin including on &#8220;Stairway to Heaven.&#8221;</p>



<p>To precisely recreate Page&#8217;s original instrument, Gibson utilized 3D scanning technology to capture its every contour before making ultra-detailed copies. </p>



<p>Gibson luthiers also visited Page at home in London to closely analyze the guitar&#8217;s specs and aging details, a process they described as taking an &#8220;MRI of the guitar.&#8221;</p>



<p></div>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-dots"/>



<p>No pricing or release date news was announced at the launch. But this guitar will not be cheap. My best guess? It&#8217;ll be north of $10,000 / £10,000. Collector models are going for around $8,000 / £8,000 on Reverb. </p>



<p>And with the Page name attached, and a likely very limited run, the new Jimmy Page 1971 Gibson EDS-1275 double neck guitar could well become one of the most expensive Gibson guitars of all time. </p>



<p></div>



<p>Page expressed enthusiasm about the Gibson partnership, saying he felt the spirit within the company. He highlighted his longstanding relationship with Gibson employees Cesar and Levin and said he was &#8220;blown away&#8221; by the firm&#8217;s attention to detail.</p>



<p>The original double neck guitar played by Page was custom-built to his specifications after Gibson discontinued the model in 1963. Its unique sound and appearance during &#8220;Stairway to Heaven&#8221; concerts cemented its legendary status.</p>



<p>While full details are still forthcoming, Gibson confirmed this is just the first release as part of its wide-ranging signature partnership with Jimmy Page, so we can definitely expect a new Page-inspired Les Paul to drop at some point this year. </p>
