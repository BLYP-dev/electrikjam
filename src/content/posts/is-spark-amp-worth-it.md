<p><strong>Positive Grid&#8217;s Spark Amp promises to bring together all of the world&#8217;s greatest amps and pedals inside one practice amp. But does it work? And, is the Spark Amp worth it? Let&#8217;s find out&#8230;</strong></p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>



<p>Transforming your novice guitar skills into something more can be frustrating, especially when your growth feels stunted by subpar equipment. </p>



<p>The majority of beginners are confined to basic practice amps that offer little to no tonal quality. While these amps serve their purpose during the initial learning stages, you&#8217;ll soon find yourself yearning for a more refined sound.</p>



<p>High-end amp stacks and <strong><a href="/boss-katana-100-mkii-review/" data-type="post" data-id="8377" data-lasso-id="235354">premium practice amps</a></strong>, however, not only put a significant dent in your wallet, but they also consume a considerable amount of space. </p>



<p>Not to mention the added complication of pedals; which ones to choose, how many to buy, the appropriate budget, and the constant debate between clone pedals and the authentic ones. </p>



<p>Plus, the cost of acquiring even a few decent pedals can be quite intimidating too. </p>



<p>Mastering the use of this equipment for the perfect tone becomes an art form in itself, and that&#8217;s before you even contemplate the intricacies of home recording. Basically, it&#8217;s a bit of a slog –&nbsp;especially for those new to the guitar. </p>



<p>But what if you could bypass all of this nonsense and go straight to a killer guitar tone, have instant access to some of the most iconic amps, effects, and pedals on the market and, best of all, do it inside a single package? </p>



<p>This is what Positive Grid&#8217;s Spark Amp sets out to do. But does it pull it off? Let&#8217;s find out&#8230; </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-is-the-spark-amp">What is The Spark Amp? </h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/is-spark-amp-worth-it-1024x683.png" alt="is spark amp worth it" class="wp-image-7870" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/is-spark-amp-worth-it-1024x683.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/02/is-spark-amp-worth-it-600x400.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/02/is-spark-amp-worth-it-300x200.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/02/is-spark-amp-worth-it-768x512.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/02/is-spark-amp-worth-it.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>The Spark Amp is often referred to as the reinvention of the practice amp. It seamlessly blends power, versatility, and convenience, offering an all-in-one solution for all your guitar-related needs. Whether you&#8217;re a beginner or a seasoned player, the Spark Amp can transform your guitar playing experience.</p>



<p>As a guitarist, space management is key. Most of us don&#8217;t have the luxury of dedicating an entire room to our gear. This is where the compact design of the Spark Amp comes in handy. </p>



<p>First unveiled at NAMM a few years ago, the Spark Amp was a game changer. Its creator, Positive Grid, known for <strong><a href="/bias-fx-vs-bias-fx-2/" data-type="post" data-id="10651" data-lasso-id="235355">the remarkable BIAS FX software</a></strong>, has outdone itself once again.</p>



<p>The Spark Amp may be small in size, but it is impressively powerful. This 40W speaker not only functions as a DAW (Digital Audio Workstation) but also offers you access to a myriad of guitar tones and effects. </p>



<p>It can be controlled via your smartphone, and it features backing tracks and AI-powered transcribing, enabling you to get tabs for all your favourite songs from YouTube.</p>



<p>When I first came across the Spark Amp, I was skeptical about its capabilities. However, upon testing it, not only was I proven wrong, but I also found myself utterly captivated by the sheer scope of what it could do. </p>



<p>The moment I plugged in my guitar, selected a guitar tone from ToneCloud (I chose one named Pike, modeled after <strong><a href="/matt-pike-guitar-guide-a-complete-list-of-what-he-uses/" data-type="post" data-id="3565" data-lasso-id="235356">Matt Pike’s guitar tone</a></strong>), I knew I was dealing with something special.</p>



<p>The Spark Amp&#8217;s compact design is a big plus. Mine sits comfortably on my desk next to my iMac, taking up minimal space. With it, I can access thousands of guitar tones, which I can further customize by adding or removing pedals and tweaking the amp settings.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="smart-jam">Smart Jam</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="745" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Is-Spark-Amp-Battery-Powered-1024x745.png" alt="Is Spark Amp Battery Powered" class="wp-image-4663" title=""></figure>



<div class="wp-block-affiliate-plugin-lasso">[lasso id=&#8221;11931&#8243; link_id=&#8221;235362&#8243; ref=&#8221;spark-40w-smart-guitar-amp-app-positive-grid&#8221; type=&#8221;button&#8221;]</div>



<p>Smart Jam is one of the most innovative and impressive features of the Spark Amp. It&#8217;s designed to make your practice sessions more engaging and interactive. The basic idea is to provide you with an AI-powered backing band that can adapt to your playing.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="getting-started-with-smart-jam">Getting Started With Smart Jam</h3>



<p>To begin, you need to connect the Spark Amp to your device (smartphone or tablet) using Bluetooth. Once connected, you&#8217;ll have to launch the Spark Amp app, where you&#8217;ll find the Smart Jam feature. In the Smart Jam section, you&#8217;ll see a list of AI drummers with different styles, like pop, rock, blues, and more. Select the drummer that suits your style or the style you want to practice.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="playing-with-smart-jam">Playing with Smart Jam</h3>



<p>Once you&#8217;ve selected your AI drummer, you&#8217;re ready to start jamming. To kick things off, you&#8217;ll need to play a chord progression or a simple rhythm for about 30 seconds. </p>



<p>The AI will &#8220;listen&#8221; to what you&#8217;re playing, then it will analyze your chords and rhythm, and create a custom backing track (drums and bass) to go along with your playing.</p>



<p>The backing track isn&#8217;t just a loop; it evolves dynamically based on your input. If you play a slow rhythm, the AI drummer and bassist will follow suit. If you speed up or switch to a different rhythm or chord progression, the AI will adapt to match your changes.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="customizing-your-jam">Customizing Your Jam</h3>



<p>What&#8217;s more, the Smart Jam feature allows you to customize the generated backing track. You can adjust the tempo and the style of the drums and bass. This flexibility allows you to experiment with different grooves and feels, helping you to improve your timing and rhythm.</p>



<p>For practice, Smart Jam is amazing –&nbsp;even more so if you&#8217;re not used to playing with other musicians. </p>
