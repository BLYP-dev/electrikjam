<p><strong>The Line 6 HX Stomp is one of the newer additions to the Helix family. It&#8217;s compact, versatile, and looks like it is packed with features. So what does it do exactly? Today we take a look at all of the features.</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator is-style-wide"/>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Line 6 HX Stomp: Helix&#8217;s Little Brother, Or Something More?</h2>



<p>Here at Electrikjam we have been <em>very</em> enamored with the<strong> <a data-lasso-id="4669" href="https://www.electrikjam.com/line-6-helix-recording-techniques/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="4209" rel="noopener">Helix</a></strong>, and all of the amazing stuff that it can do. There are tons of amp models, speaker cabs, microphone placement options&#8230;the possibilities are really limited only to your imagination. For some people this might be a little overwhelming. I totally understand that!</p>



<p>When it comes to the bigger<a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/expensive-amp-simulators/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="2801" rel="noopener" data-lasso-id="5255"> <strong>amp sims</strong></a> and products like them, I often get option paralysis. It&#8217;s nice to have so many options and effects right at your fingertips, but I am one of those people that will sit there and play with the settings and never actually record anything.</p>



<p>So I need&#8230;<strong> <em>Limits.</em></strong></p>



<p>I imagine a lot of other people look at the Helix like that as well. More importantly, some people just want to use their own amp, and use the collection of Helix effects with their rig. because while the <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/neural-amp-modeler/" data-lasso-id="223248">amp models and cabinet sims</a> are all top notch, the real hero of the Line 6 products is the amazing collection of digital effects.</p>



<p>I think this is where the Line6 HX Stomp comes in. This is for the people that want a great set of amp sims and effects, but they don&#8217;t <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/line-6-helix-lt-reviews/" data-lasso-id="6237">need something like the full-on Helix</a>. Today we are going to take a look at the <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/is-line-6-hx-stomp-worth-it/" data-lasso-id="9712">Line 6 HX Stomp</a>, and all of it&#8217;s features, while comparing it to it&#8217;s big brother.</p>

The knobs will seems familiar to Line 6 users, along with the three foot pedals. On the surface, it looks like a baby Helix, and to be honest, that&#8217;s kind of what it is!<br></p>

<p>Let&#8217;s start with the most obvious thing, the size. This thing is tiny! It is about the size of a paperback book. This was designed to be used on your<strong> <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/building-your-first-pedalboard-the-easy-step-by-step-guide/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="1087" rel="noopener" data-lasso-id="5256">pedalboard</a></strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/building-your-first-pedalboard-the-easy-step-by-step-guide/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="1087" rel="noopener" data-lasso-id="5257">,</a> and it takes up a smaller foot print than some <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/the-best-guitar-pedals-1968-2021/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="2863" rel="noopener" data-lasso-id="5258"><strong>overdrive pedals</strong></a> that I can think of. If you plan on using the Line 6 HX Stomp to record, it is perfect to sit on your desk. It takes up almost zero space.</p>



<p>It does&#8230;a lot!</p>

<p>We have talked about how the Helix can be used as a stand-alone <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/how-to-record-electric-guitar-at-home-guide/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="1193" rel="noopener" data-lasso-id="5260"><strong>interface</strong></a> before, and the Line 6 HX Stomp can do the same thing! You can do this by either plugging the HX Stomp directly into an interface, or you can use it via USB and make it an interface! It has all of the same routing options as well. </p>



<p>It works just like the Helix when in USB mode. This means you can record, but you can also edit patches in real-time with <a data-lasso-id="5261" href="https://line6.com/software/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"><strong>HX Edit.</strong></a> Just like the Helix, you technically never have to touch the knobs to program all of your patches. The Line 6 HX Stomp can also download user patches from the huge library on the Line 6 forums. They have everything from <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/new-metallica-album-release-date-new-songs-update/" data-lasso-id="17577">Metallica</a> to Eddie Van Halen, and the sounds are very good quality!</p>



<p>The input/output options on the rear of the unit are definitely less than the Helix. However, the back of the unit has everything you need to plug up to AUX, USB, or typical speaker cable methods. But it does not have anywhere near the options that the Helix has. The Line 6 HX Stomp has everything <em>most</em> people would<em> need,</em> though.</p>



<p>It has some limitations compared to the Helix, but that is by design. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Line 6 Helix vs Line 6 HX Stomp</h2>



<p>The HX Stomp has some serious limitations compared to the flagship Helix. Firstly, you can only have 8 blocks of effects, unlike the 32 blocks you get with the Helix. For some people, this is going to be a deal-breaker. Tons of guitarists tweak their Helix to the max with subtle effects and add-ons. The HX Stomp is not going to work for that.</p>



<p>There is also a lack of expression pedal on the HX Stomp. Again, for some people, this will be a deal-breaker. The Helix has the expression pedal to control wah-wah effects, values, and volume controls. Now, you CAN buy an <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/line-6-pod-express-review/" data-lasso-id="263283">expression pedal and hook it up to the Line</a> 6 HX Stomp unit, or use one that you already have. But the Helix clearly wins this category.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-full"><img decoding="async" width="900" height="534" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/rear-Hx-stomp.png" alt="" class="wp-image-4299" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/rear-Hx-stomp.png 900w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/rear-Hx-stomp-600x356.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/rear-Hx-stomp-300x178.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/rear-Hx-stomp-768x456.png 768w" sizes="(max-width: 900px) 100vw, 900px" /><figcaption><em><strong>HX Stomp Rear Panel</strong></em></figcaption></figure>



<p>The Helix also has &#8220;bank&#8221; buttons on the side of the unit, that lets you easily scroll through your presets. The HX Stomp is missing these and only has the three-button switches. The three switches on the HX Stomp can be assigned to do a lot of things, like turn effects on/off, and set up a tuner function. But it lacks all of the options that the Helix has for tuning patches and &#8220;scenes&#8221; on and off.</p>



<p>But at the core of the HX Stomp, it still uses the same sounds and effects that the Helix uses. The same quality, and options. Just&#8230;a bit limited. The HX Stomp is half the price of the Helix as well. The limitations of the HX Stomp are purposeful, though. because it was designed to be used in a completely different way than the Helix.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator is-style-wide"/>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Line 6 HX Stomp: The New Core of Your Pedalboard</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="576" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/HX-Stomp-on-pedalboard-1024x576.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-4295" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/HX-Stomp-on-pedalboard-1024x576.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/HX-Stomp-on-pedalboard-600x337.jpg 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/HX-Stomp-on-pedalboard-300x169.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/HX-Stomp-on-pedalboard-768x432.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/HX-Stomp-on-pedalboard-1080x607.jpg 1080w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/HX-Stomp-on-pedalboard.jpg 1234w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /><figcaption><em><strong>The HX Stomp on a board</strong></em></figcaption></figure>



<p>The HX Stomp is limited in design, but that&#8217;s because it is supposed to function as an addition to your existing pedalboard. Even though, technically, it has everything you need to replace your traditional rig. </p>



<p><strong>There are a couple of ways you can do this</strong>. The first is to use the HX Stomp as a stand-alone effects unit. It is easy to go into the settings and remove all of the <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/nux-mighty-amps-review/" data-lasso-id="159877">amp models</a>. That means you have thousands of effects combos that you can incorporate into your rig. You can still use your amplifier as the main source of your tone, and use the HX Stomp for the amazing delay, chorus, octave, and other arrays of effects. </p>



<p>The second way you can add the HX Stomp to your pedalboard is to use it as the &#8220;brain&#8221; of your board. You can use the amp models, and effects as your primary tone source, and run the whole rig through an <a data-lasso-id="5262" href="https://www.electrikjam.com/what-is-an-frfr-speaker-cabinet-pros-cons/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="4226" rel="noopener"><strong>FRFR cab</strong></a>. With this method, you can also use your other pedals to color the tone. Maybe you have a<strong> <a data-lasso-id="5263" href="https://www.electrikjam.com/best-guitar-pedals-for-doom-metal/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="1153" rel="noopener">fuzz pedal</a> </strong>that sounds <em>so good</em>, that the Helix can&#8217;t replicate it. You can still use that pedal in conjunction with your HX Stomp.</p>



<p>This is exactly what the HX Stomp was designed to be! The balanced dual effects loop line out allows you to place the unit anywhere in your pedal chain. You can use it for a wide variety of effects, or just a few carefully programmed effects that are tailored to your setlist. Likewise, you can also use it as your sole effects pedal, plugged into your amp&#8217;s effects loop.</p>



<p>But these are just the basic ways you can use the HX Stomp with your live rig. The HX Stomp allows to you to be as restricted, or brilliantly complicated as you want. There are tons of ways you can integrate it into your rig. Or, if you want&#8230;it can replace your amp rig! </p>



<p>But the HX Stomp is not just for live uses. It can also be a<em> huge</em> advantage in a studio situation.</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator is-style-wide"/>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Using The Line 6 HX Stomp For Recording</h2>



<p>Just like the Helix, the HX Stomp can be used as a recording interface without any other hardware. It can be used to be the interface between your computer and DAW. This can be done as a dry signal, where you can re-amp the track, or it can be used with all of the HX Effects and amp sims. There are tons of ways to route the HX Stomp.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="525" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/usb-audio-hx-effects-dry-di-1024x525.png" alt="" class="wp-image-4297" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/usb-audio-hx-effects-dry-di-1024x525.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/usb-audio-hx-effects-dry-di-600x308.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/usb-audio-hx-effects-dry-di-300x154.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/usb-audio-hx-effects-dry-di-768x394.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/usb-audio-hx-effects-dry-di.png 1072w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /><figcaption><em><strong>Using the HX Stomp As An Interface</strong></em></figcaption></figure>



<p>This is another way that the size of the HX Stomp can be a huge advantage. It is very small and can be used as a hub on your desktop to <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/must-have-for-recording-guitar/" data-lasso-id="9534">record guitar</a>. Just like the Helix, you can also use the HX Stomp to record other instruments as well, including vocals and keyboards. And you can do this with or without the HX Effects! We went over how to do this with the Helix<a data-lasso-id="5264" href="https://www.electrikjam.com/line-6-helix-recording-techniques/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="4209" rel="noopener"><strong> in detail</strong></a>. It works the same with the HX Stomp.</p>



<p>The interface feature is not just an &#8220;added on&#8221; feature that was some sort of afterthought. The HX Stomp uses all of the same USB recording features as the Helix. This feature was part of the design from the ground up. </p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator is-style-wide"/>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Is The Line 6 HX Stomp Worth It? Closing Thoughts&#8230;</h2>



<p>Line 6 is at the forefront of modeling technology and digital effects. The HX Stomp was clearly designed to fit a niche that the Helix cannot. It is the compact solution to recording, adding to your effects chain, or using it as a stand-alone unit. The possibilities are crazy!</p>



<p>The HX Stomp is also a cheaper option than the Helix for someone on a budget. The helix is a powerhouse that is meant to be an all-in-one solution to your guitar rig, but the price reflects that! The HX Stomp is half the price –&nbsp;<strong><a data-lasso-id="5696" data-lasso-name="Line 6 HX Stomp" href="https://www.electrikjam.com/line-6-helix-stomp-pedal/" data-type="lasso-urls" data-id="4345" rel="nofollow noopener" target="_blank">it retails for $599</a></strong> –&nbsp;and is even cheaper if you look at the used market.</p>



<p>So how you want to use the HX Stomp is up to you. But there is no denying that big things definitely can come in small packages!</p>

<div id="faq-question-1631807987680" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">Does The Line 6 HX Stomp have all of the Amp Models as the Helix?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>Yes. The HX Stomp uses the same technology as the Helix series, and uses the HX Edit program just like the Helix.</p>

</div>
</div>
<div id="faq-question-1631808055858" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">Does The Line 6 HX Stomp have all of the same effects as the Helix?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>The HX Stomp has all of the same quality effects as the Line 6 Helix. The only difference is how many you can program at once. The HX Stomp has 8 blocks max, while the Helix has up to 32.</p>

</div>
</div>
<div id="faq-question-1631808150637" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">Can I transfer HX Stomp Patches to my Helix?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>Yes. You can use the HX Edit program to transfer all of your patches back and forth between the two units. It also works with Helix Native plugins. </p>

</div>
</div>
<div id="faq-question-1631808218223" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">Do I need an interface to record with the Line 6 HX Stomp?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>You do not! You can use the HX Stomp as a stand-alone interface to record ad dry signal, or record using the patches and presets on the unit. The unit connects via USB directly to your computer and can be used with your favorite DAW.</p>
