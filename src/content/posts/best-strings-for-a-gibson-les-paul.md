<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How To Choose The Best Strings For Your Gibson Les Paul (Or Any Les Paul Style Guitar)</h2>

<p>Choosing guitar strings can feel weirdly personal, right? What works great for one player might feel totally wrong to another. That’s especially true with a guitar like the Gibson Les Paul, where string gauge can totally change the feel and response of the instrument.</p>



<p>So how do you figure out which strings are right for you?</p>



<p>Well, string gauge is one of those never-ending debates in the guitar world. Everyone has an opinion, and they’ll defend it like their life depends on it. But instead of just arguing about it on forums, I thought we’d take a more practical look.</p>



<p>Today, I’ll walk you through three of the most popular string sets people use on Les Paul-style guitars. </p>



<p>These picks are based on my own personal experience (good ol’ trial and error, basically). And yeah—Option #1 is my personal go-to. </p>



<p>The other two? I’ll break them down as fairly as possible so you can decide what works best for you.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Scale Length</h3>



<p>The biggest factor in determining what gauge strings you use, should be the scale length of your guitar. Different companies use a varying array of scale lengths:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list is-style-list-check">
<li>Fender: 25.5 Inches</li>



<li>Paul Reed Smith: 25 Inches</li>



<li>Gibson: 24.75 Inches</li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What is scale length, though?</strong></h4>



<p>Scale length is described as the distance the string travels between the <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/anatomy-of-the-electric-guitar-guide/" data-type="post" data-id="450" data-lasso-id="173"><strong>nut and the bridge</strong>.</a> This effects the tension of the strings, and the space between frets as well. </p>



<p>Everyone eventually either finds that they prefer a certain scale length, or conversely, they decide that they really don&#8217;t have a preference! I have never met a guitar that I didn&#8217;t like a little bit.</p>



<p><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/is-a-gibson-les-paul-worth-it-my-2-cents/" data-lasso-id="44931">Gibson Les Paul&#8217;s</a> ship with a brand new with a set of .009- .046 gauge strings. But that doesn&#8217;t mean that you have to use that set of strings with your Gibson Les Paul. In fact, that is not the most popular gauge.</p>
