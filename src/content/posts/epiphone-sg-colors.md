<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Which Epiphone SG Comes In The Most Colors?</strong></h2>



<p>If you want the absolute most choice with colors, you’re best going with the Epiphone SG Muse – it is available in six unique color options: <strong>Radio Blue Metallic, Pearl White Metallic, Purple Passion Metallic, Wanderlust Green Metallic, Jet Black Metallic, and Scarlett Red Metallic.</strong></p>



			
			<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="576" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/epiphone-sg-muse-1024x576.jpg" alt="epiphone sg muse" class="wp-image-4162" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/epiphone-sg-muse-1024x576.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/epiphone-sg-muse-600x338.jpg 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/epiphone-sg-muse-300x169.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/epiphone-sg-muse-768x432.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/epiphone-sg-muse-1080x608.jpg 1080w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/epiphone-sg-muse.jpg 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /><figcaption><strong>The Epiphone SG Muse – <a href="/recommends/epiphone-sg-muse-electric-guitar/" data-lasso-id="3481">Yours For Just $429</a></strong></figcaption></figure>
			
		


<hr class="wp-block-separator"/>



<p>The Epiphone SG Muse is also very well priced too at $429. The guitar features a classic mahogany body with a 24.75&#8243; scale length. With the neck, it uses a Custom C mahogany neck and Indian laurel fretboard. With pickups, you’re looking at a pair of Alnico Classic PRO humbuckers with coil-splitting and phase controls.</p>



<p>The SG Muse also comes with a LockTone ABR bridge, stopbar tailpiece, and Grover 18:1 tuners that’ll ensure the guitar stays in tune for longer which is super important if you plan on using the Epiphone SG Muse to play live or record.</p>



<p>If you’re <strong><a href="/best-guitars-for-metal/" data-type="post" data-id="906" data-lasso-id="3482">looking for a guitar to play metal</a></strong>, or anything fast, the Epiphone SG Muse is a great option, thanks to its Custom C mahogany neck that&#8217;ll help you shred through riffs and scales with ease and precision. The Epiphone SG Muse is a brilliant piece of kit, a welcome addition to the classic SG stable.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What is The Best Epiphone SG For The Money?</strong></h2>



<p>If you’re after the best possible specs and performance from Epiphone’s SG current range of models, the Epiphone SG Prophecy is currently the most expensive option. <strong><a href="/recommends/epiphone-sg-prophecy-electric-guitar/" data-lasso-id="3483">And in my opinion, it is the best too</a></strong> – especially if you want to play metal and/or hard rock.</p>



			
			<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-full"><img decoding="async" width="638" height="652" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/epiphone-sg-prophecy-colors.png" alt="" class="wp-image-4163" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/epiphone-sg-prophecy-colors.png 638w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/epiphone-sg-prophecy-colors-600x613.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/09/epiphone-sg-prophecy-colors-294x300.png 294w" sizes="(max-width: 638px) 100vw, 638px" /><figcaption><strong>The <meta charset="utf-8">Epiphone SG Prophecy – <a href="/recommends/epiphone-sg-prophecy-electric-guitar/" data-lasso-id="3484">The Best Modern SG Model | From $899</a></strong></figcaption></figure>
			
		


<hr class="wp-block-separator"/>



<p>What makes the Epiphone SG Prophecy so special? Simple: the Epiphone SG Prophecy has all the usual SG stuff you know and love. But it has a new, modern twist with its <strong><a href="/fishman-fluence-modern-review-is-the-hype-legit/" data-type="post" data-id="716" data-lasso-id="3485">Fishman Fluence pickups</a></strong>. These pickups are brilliant for metal and hard rock as they’re hum and noise-free, so the <a href="/fuzz-vs-distortion/" data-lasso-id="36508">fuzz and distortion</a> are heavy as hell but super-precise.</p>



<p>Fishman’s pickups have become incredibly popular of late with metal players. If you’re playing heavy music, like metal, you want the guitars to sound huge. But you do not want any details getting lost in noise or hum. This is where Fishman Fluence pickups come into play: <strong>you get all the heaviness and outstanding tone, just without any of the noise and waste.</strong></p>
