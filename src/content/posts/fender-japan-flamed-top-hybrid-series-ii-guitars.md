<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-left has-base-color has-text-color">Fender Japan Debuts New Hybrid Series II Finishes&#8230;</h2>



<p>Key Takeaways:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Fender Japan introduces two new finishes for Hybrid Series II: &#8220;Twilight&#8221; and &#8220;Sunset Orange Metallic&#8221;</li>



<li>New finishes blend traditional designs with modern aesthetics</li>



<li>Available on Stratocaster, Telecaster, Jazzmaster, Jazz Bass, and a unique HSS model</li>



<li>Features include quilted maple tops and Orange Drop capacitors</li>



<li>Offers vintage-inspired builds with modern specifications</li>



<li>Pricing starts at approximately $945 (150,700 Japanese Yen)</li>



<li>Targets players seeking distinctive looks without compromising classic Fender appeal</li>
</ul>

<p>Hold onto your neon leg warmers and dust off your lava lamps, folks, because Fender Japan is at it again! </p>



<p>The mad scientists of the guitar world have birthed a new litter of six-string wonders that&#8217;ll make your grandpappy&#8217;s Strat blush and your Instagram feed explode.</p>



<p>Fender Japan has expanded its Hybrid Series II electric guitars with two new eye-catching finishes: &#8220;Twilight&#8221; and &#8220;Sunset Orange Metallic.&#8221;</p>

<p>And the result? They’re utterly gorgeous, illustrating just how much a difference a lick of paint can make when it comes to guitars.</p>



<p>Given Fender’s massive resurgence in recent years and its enduring popularity with players from all types of genres, stuff like this is just great to see –&nbsp;you just simply wouldn’t get anything like this from another legacy brand.</p>



<p>The whole “ethos” with the Hybrid Series is about blending traditional Fender designs with modern aesthetics.</p>



<p>The &#8220;Twilight&#8221; finish features a dramatic gradient from deep blue to vibrant orange, creating a striking visual effect. &#8220;Sunset Orange Metallic&#8221; offers a contemporary twist on the classic burst finish, with warm, fiery tones.</p>



<p></div>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Fender Japan Hybrid Series II Lineup</strong></h2>



<p>These new finishes are available across the Hybrid Series II lineup, which includes:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Stratocaster</strong></li>



<li><strong>Telecaster</strong></li>



<li><strong>Jazzmaster</strong></li>



<li><strong>Jazz Bass</strong></li>



<li><strong>A unique HSS model reminiscent of Yamaha&#8217;s high-end Pacifica Professional</strong></li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Key features of the Hybrid Series II:</strong></h3>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Quilted maple tops</strong></li>



<li><strong>Orange Drop capacitors</strong></li>



<li><strong>Various pickup configurations for versatility</strong></li>



<li><strong>Vintage-inspired builds with modern specifications</strong></li>
</ul>



<p>The series also includes the notable Larimar Telecaster, featuring an ethereal finish inspired by a rare Dominican gemstone.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How Much Are Fender Japan’s Hybrid Series II Guitars?</strong></h2>



<p>Pricing for the Hybrid Series II starts at 150,700 Japanese Yen (approximately $945), which is round about what you&#8217;ll pay for a stock Fender here in the US, so that&#8217;s notable. </p>
