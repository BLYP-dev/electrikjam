<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="fender-player-stratocaster-still-the-best-value">Fender Player Stratocaster: Still The Best Value?</h2>



<p>***<strong>Fender has now updated the OG Player Stratocaster; read <a href="/fender-player-ii-stratocaster-reviews/">my full review of the Fender Player II Stratocaster </a>and find out why the new series is so much better&#8230;</strong></p>



<p>Over the years, the &#8220;Made In Mexico&#8221; Fender guitars have been looked at as the best value for a genuine Fender. Guitarists have always flocked to the <a data-lasso-id="131241" href="/recommends/fender-player-telecaster/" data-type="post" data-id="8287" target="_blank" rel="noopener">MIM Fenders</a> as &#8220;workhorse guitars&#8221; that are great for the stage and studio. </p>



<p>In fact, many players will tell you that there is not that big of a difference between an American Standard and a Mexican Made Fender.</p>



<p>And we’ll cover why below, including an explanation on how the Fender Player is different from the Fender Standard series, what to expect regarding improvements in quality control and overall features, and why a Player series Fender is probably a better option than a straight-up US-made Fender Strat.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="mim-fender-strat-vs-american-fender-strat"><strong>MIM Fender Strat vs American Fender Strat </strong></h3>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-1024x683.png" alt="" class="wp-image-10884" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-1024x683.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-600x400.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-300x200.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-768x512.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>The thing about this big myth of &#8220;American VS MIM&#8221; is that it DOES have some truth behind it. The two factories are <em>right next</em> to each other, and they often share the same parts (Bodies, necks, hardware). </p>



<p>When I say that the Mexican factory is<em> close</em>, I mean by a few <em>miles</em>. So the question has always been &#8220;Should I buy a MIM, or a USA?&#8221;. </p>



<p>I mean, a Stratocaster is a modular instrument, it was designed to be that way. </p>



<p>It was made to be easy to repair, affordable for musicians, and it became iconic for these reasons. A Stratocaster is a <em>simple</em> guitar, construction-wise.</p>



<p>Over the years I have owned both, and my favorite Fender guitars have almost all been MIM. The Fender Player Stratocaster replaced the &#8220;Standard&#8221; MIM series only by name. </p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="new-player-series-features"><strong>New &#8220;Player Series&#8221; Features</strong></h3>



<p>The quality that we all know is still there. In fact, there are some upgraded features like the synthetic bone nut that make the Player Series <em>better</em> than the older MIM Standards. </p>



<p>So is it still worth it to buy the Fender Player Stratocaster? I think so, and the rest of the internet seems to agree with me.</p>



<p>The Fender Player Stratocaster didn&#8217;t just change its name from &#8220;Standard&#8221;. There were tons of upgrades with the Player Series. </p>



<p>You got better pickups, a two point trem, better nut, and more color options. </p>



<p>These were usually the first things that people <em>changed</em> about the MIM Standard Series, so it saves you money in the long run! </p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-locking-tuners-1024x683.png" alt="Fender Player Stratocaster locking tuners" class="wp-image-10885" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-locking-tuners-1024x683.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-locking-tuners-600x400.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-locking-tuners-300x200.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-locking-tuners-768x512.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-locking-tuners.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>The complete overhaul of the Mexican line was a <em>big</em> move for Fender, but it was a long time coming.</p>



<p>It is not a coincidence either that after the change, Fender had <a data-lasso-id="131328" href="/fender-guitar-sales-in-2020-are-insane-more-people-playing-guitar-during-pandemic/" data-type="post" data-id="1475" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>record breaking sales</strong></a>. Now of course this also had a lot to do with lockdowns, but the sales figures actually started <em>before</em> the pandemic. </p>



<p>So Fender was on its way to a record breaking year before lockdowns <em>even began</em>. </p>



<p>I think this is a &#8220;perfect storm&#8221; scenario for Fender. </p>



<p>Other than lockdowns, more people are learning guitar now. The guitar is a popular instrument, and Fender is a household name.</p>



<p>The few times that <a data-lasso-id="213186" href="/fender-meteora-players-plus-review-2022/">I have sprung for a USA Fender</a>, I didn&#8217;t feel like it was worth the price. </p>



<p>Yes, a few features are nicer. </p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="why-mexican-strats-are-cheaper"><strong>Why Mexican Strats Are Cheaper</strong></h3>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Strat-1024x683.png" alt="what is the best electric guitar brand?" class="wp-image-10550" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Strat-1024x683.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Strat-600x400.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Strat-300x200.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Strat-768x512.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Strat.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>But sometimes I feel like you&#8217;re just paying for the &#8220;USA&#8221; stamp on the neck.  The Player Series solved most of the QC issues that Fender had with the MIM lineup. </p>



<p>So these days, I almost always recommend a Player Series over the USA counterpart. </p>



<p>Believe me, I professionally toured a $400 MIM Stratocaster for <em>YEARS</em>, and it <em>never</em> let me down.</p>



<p>So is the Fender Player Stratocaster still worth it in 2022? I think it is still a great buy for anyone wanting the entry-level Fender that has tons of features to offer. </p>



<p>The Player Series is still the workhorse guitar that will be great for playing shows, studio work, and even <a data-lasso-id="131243" href="/guitar-intervals-for-beginners/" data-type="post" data-id="8221" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>beginners</strong></a>. </p>



<p>But don&#8217;t just trust my word. The internet is full of praise for The Player Series Stratocaster.</p>

<p>When it comes to pickups, the vast majority –&nbsp;or the most popular models – in Fender’s Player Stratocaster series come with the classic SSS configuration which is made up of Fender Player Series Alnico V pickups and CTS pots for the volume and tone controls.</p>

<p>Again, for that classic Strat sound, you’ll want to go with <strong>the classic SSS configuration</strong>. But if you do plan on doing anything close to metal or stoner rock and you want to create heavier, lower tones, one of Fender’s HSS Player Strats will definitely get the job done.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-are-hss-and-sss-pickups"><strong>What are HSS and SSS Pickups?</strong></h3>



<p>Before we get into the differences between HSS and SSS pickups, let&#8217;s first define what they are. </p>



<p>Single-coil pickups are made up of a single coil of wire wrapped around a magnet. They produce a bright, clear tone that is ideal for clean playing styles like blues and country.</p>



<p>Humbucker pickups, on the other hand, use two coils wired together in series to cancel out unwanted noise (like hum) while boosting output levels. </p>



<p>They produce a thicker, warmer tone that is well-suited to heavier genres like rock and metal.</p>



<p><strong>The Fender Player Stratocaster comes with either three single-coil pickups (SSS) or two single-coils and one humbucker (HSS).</strong></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="comparing-hss-and-sss-pickups-on-the-fender-player-stratocaster"><strong>Comparing HSS and SSS Pickups on the Fender Player Stratocaster</strong></h3>



<p>When it comes to sound characteristics, there are some key differences between HSS and SSS configurations. </p>



<p>The main advantage of an SSS setup is its tonal versatility. With three single-coil pickups, you have access to a wide range of sounds from bright and twangy to warm and mellow.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-HSS-vs-SSS-1024x683.png" alt="Fender Player Stratocaster HSS vs SSS" class="wp-image-10887" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-HSS-vs-SSS-1024x683.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-HSS-vs-SSS-600x400.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-HSS-vs-SSS-300x200.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-HSS-vs-SSS-768x512.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-HSS-vs-SSS.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>An HSS configuration offers more output levels than an SSS setup thanks to the addition of a humbucker pickup. This makes it ideal for players who want to play heavier genres like hard rock or metal without sacrificing clarity or sustain.</p>



<p>Ultimately, choosing between an HSS or SSS configuration will depend on your personal preference as well as your playing style. </p>



<p>If you&#8217;re primarily playing clean tones or lighter genres like blues or jazz, an SSS configuration may be best suited for you. </p>



<p>However, if you prefer heavier genres like hard rock or metal but still want access to cleaner tones when needed, an HSS configuration may be the way to go.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="hss-vs-sss-pickups-pros-cons">HSS vs SSS Pickups: PROS &amp; CONS</h3>



<p>Here&#8217;s a breakdown of some pros and cons associated with each type:</p>



<p><strong>Pros of an SSS Configuration</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Brighter tone</li>



<li>More tonal versatility</li>



<li>Less expensive</li>
</ul>



<p><strong>Cons of an SSS Configuration</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Prone to noise/hum interference</li>



<li>Not ideal for high-gain distortion</li>
</ul>



<p><strong>Pros of an HHS Configuration</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Higher output levels</li>



<li>Better noise reduction thanks to humbucker pickup</li>



<li>Ideal for harder genres like heavy rock/metal</li>
</ul>



<p><strong>Cons of an HHS Configuration</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Less tonal versatility compared to an SSS setup</li>



<li>More expensive due to added humbucker pickup</li>
</ul>



<p>In conclusion, choosing between an HHS or SSB configuration ultimately depends on your personal preference as well as your playing style. </p>



<p>If you&#8217;re looking for tonal versatility with brighter tones then choose an SS pick-up configuration but if you&#8217;re looking for higher output levels with better noise reduction then opt-in for HH pick-up configuration.</p>
