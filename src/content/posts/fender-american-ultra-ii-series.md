<p>Whether you&#8217;re a gigging pro, a tone chaser, or just someone who wants a versatile axe with killer looks, the Fender Ultra II series has something for you. </p>



<p>Fender didn&#8217;t just slap on a new coat of paint on these models either; this lineup builds on the original Ultra series with some major upgrades.</p>



<p>So, what&#8217;s new with the Ultra II? Let’s dive in.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Ultra II Upgrades: What’s Different?</strong></h3>



<p>The American Ultra II series doesn’t mess around when it comes to improvements. </p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote has-x-small-font-size is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p><strong>Built for players boldly seeking the best, American Ultra II is our most advanced series of instruments. American Ultra II guitars and basses feature premium materials, precision craftsmanship and cutting-edge design for instruments that both advance the state-of-the-art and stand the test of time.</strong></p>



<p><a href="https://www.fender.com/en-GB/american-ultra-ii-series.html/?dc&amp;gad_source=1&amp;gclid=Cj0KCQiAo5u6BhDJARIsAAVoDWtkU8UmGxb0joRwV1YkYh6QjgZx926v212V20AtQTNeb7Wvg8As2cQaAnzSEALw_wcB" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" data-lasso-id="270510">Fender</a></p>
</blockquote>



<p>Here are the standout features that set it apart from its predecessor:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Quartersawn Maple Necks</strong>: Forget about neck warping or stability issues. These necks are designed to be ultra-reliable and smooth to play. Perfect for those marathon gigs.</li>



<li><strong>Ultra II Noiseless Pickups and Haymaker Humbucker</strong>: The Ultra II series features new Noiseless pickups in Hot and Vintage variants, along with a Haymaker humbucker for a tone range that spans vintage to modern. Say goodbye to unwanted hum and hello to versatility.</li>



<li><strong>Ebony Fingerboards</strong>: Now you can choose between maple or ebony fingerboards. Ebony brings a slightly brighter tone and a slicker playing feel.</li>



<li><strong>S-1 Switching, Revamped</strong>: Fender has overhauled the S-1 switching system, giving you even more tonal options. It’s a tweak that’s perfect for studio nerds and stage players alike.</li>



<li><strong>Graph Tech TUSQ Nuts</strong>: These nuts improve tuning stability, making them a win for players who bend strings like crazy.</li>



<li><strong>Luminlay Side Dots</strong>: A Fender first! These glow-in-the-dark side dots are a lifesaver when playing under low lighting.</li>



<li><strong>New Finishes and Anodized Pickguards</strong>: Fender added four car-inspired finishes that make these guitars look just as good as they sound. Pair that with anodized aluminum pickguards, and you’ve got a head-turner.</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Final Thoughts: Is the Ultra II Worth It?</strong></h3>



<p>The Fender American Ultra II series takes everything great about the original Ultra and makes it better.</p>



<p>From the modern necks to the glow-in-the-dark details, these guitars are built for players who demand performance and style. </p>



<p>The addition of the Meteora is the cherry on top, giving us a unique option in Fender’s already stacked lineup.</p>



<div class="wp-block-group has-border-color has-border-light-border-color has-global-padding is-layout-constrained wp-container-core-group-is-layout-64671f1d wp-block-group-is-layout-constrained" style="border-radius:10px;padding-top:var(--wp--preset--spacing--large);padding-right:var(--wp--preset--spacing--large);padding-bottom:var(--wp--preset--spacing--large);padding-left:var(--wp--preset--spacing--large)">
<p class="has-condensed-font-family has-medium-font-size" style="font-style:normal;font-weight:600">Key Takeaways&#8230;</p>



<p><strong>Why is the Meteora a big deal?</strong><br>It’s the first time this bold model is offered as a USA-made guitar, bringing top-tier quality to its unique design.</p>



<p><strong>What’s new in the Ultra II series?</strong><br>Key upgrades include quartersawn maple necks, Ultra II Noiseless pickups, Haymaker humbuckers, ebony fingerboards, and glow-in-the-dark Luminlay side dots.</p>



<p><strong>What’s the difference between the Ultra and Ultra II?</strong><br>The Ultra II refines the original with better pickups, more ergonomic body contours, and upgraded materials like Graph Tech nuts.</p>
