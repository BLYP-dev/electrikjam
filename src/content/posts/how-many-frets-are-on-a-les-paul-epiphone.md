The answer is almost always 22 frets. This is due to Epiphone being modeled after Gibson guitars.</strong></p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How Many Frets Are On A Les Paul Epiphone? And Why?</h2>



<p>Les Paul models are known for having 22 frets. This is the same whether we are talking about the <a href="/adam-jones-epiphone-1979-les-paul-custom-release-date-specs/" data-lasso-id="148">Epiphone Les Paul</a>, or it&#8217;s big brother Gibson. This comes from the <a href="/who-invented-the-electric-guitar/" data-type="post" data-id="583" data-lasso-id="149">history of the electric guitar</a>, and the heritage. </p>



<p><strong><a href="/epiphone-sg-vs-epiphone-les-paul/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="150">Both the SG and Les Paul</a></strong> both use 22 frets, in fact. Although <strong><a class="rank-math-link" href="/epiphone-sg-standard-vs-epiphone-sg-custom/" data-lasso-id="151">Epiphone (and Gibson&#8217;s) SG guitars</a></strong> are quite a bit lighter. </p>



<p>Early electric guitars used 22 frets as a standard due to the scale length of most guitars. These days, many guitars have 24 frets and a much longer scale length. This gives you a slightly higher range and more space to <a href="/how-to-find-fretboard-notes-guitar/" data-lasso-id="152">play on the fretboard</a>.</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li><strong><a href="/best-cases-for-epiphone-les-paul/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="153">Gear Guide: The Best Epiphone Les Paul Cases: My #1 Picks&#8230;</a></strong></li></ul>



<p><strong>Traditional 22 frets and 24.75 Scale length:</strong> <strong><a href="/gibson-guitars/" data-type="page" data-id="3878" data-lasso-id="2603">Gibson</a></strong> and <strong><a href="/epiphone-guitars/" data-lasso-id="2604">Epiphone</a></strong> alike are known for making &#8220;classic&#8221; instruments. This means that over the years, the specs and features have rarely changed since their inception in the 1950&#8217;s.</p>



<p>The first <a href="/types-of-guitars-guide/" data-lasso-id="154">Les Paul models</a> in production used this shorter scale and 22 frets. It is pretty cool that the Les Paul remains popular &#8220;as is&#8221;. <a href="/gibson-vs-epiphone-in-2021/" data-lasso-id="155">Epiphone builds all of their guitars to Gibson specification</a> to this day and it remains one of the most popular guitars on the planet!</p>

<p>While 24 fret guitars dominate the metal market, many professional players still use 22 fret guitars. Take a look at Slash or <a href="/zakk-wylde-quotes-about-life-guitars-black-label-society/" data-lasso-id="156">Zakk Wylde</a>! They both shred with 22 fret guitars, on their Les Paul models. </p>



<p>So how many frets are on a <a href="/best-epiphone-les-paul-models/" data-lasso-id="5809">Les Paul Epiphone</a>? Always 22, due to the fact that they still follows historically accurate blueprints for their guitars! <a href="/best-les-paul-copy/" data-lasso-id="4658">Playing a Les Paul</a> is a lot like playing a piece of history.</p>



<p>So while 24 fret guitars are popular, I don&#8217;t think we will ever see 22 fret guitars go away. <strong><a href="/best-guitar-for-beginners/" data-type="post" data-id="505" data-lasso-id="157">Epiphone Les Paul models</a></strong> are played by guitarists in every genre of music, in every style. </p>
