<p><strong>Zakk Wylde has been a consistent presence in the metal scene for over three decades. From doing his own thing with Black Label Society to playing with Ozzy and battling his own demons, the man is a living legend. And here’s some of Zakk Wylde’s best quotes…</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator is-style-wide"/>



<p>Born Zachary Phillip Wylde, the man and legend we now know as Zakk Wylde grew up in Bayonne, New Jersey. Born January 14, 1967, Wylde began playing guitar at the age of eight years old. By the time he was in high school, he was playing non-stop between getting home from school and the next morning.</p>



<p>Wylde graduated high school in 1985. His first band was called <strong><em>Stone Henge</em></strong>, a local Jersey band. He then formed another band called <strong><em>Zyris</em></strong>. His big break, however, came when he landed lead guitar in <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/doom-metal-beginners-guide/" data-type="post" data-id="1042" data-lasso-id="1569">Ozzy Osbourne’s band</a></strong>, replacing Brad Gillis, who had previously replaced the legendary Randy Rhodes.</p>



<p>Wylde also briefly played with <strong><em>Guns N Roses</em></strong> too, following an audition in 1995. During this time, Zakk was replaced in Ozzy’s band by Joe Holmes in 1995 until his return in 2001. During this time, Wylde founded <strong><em>Black Label Society</em></strong>; its first album, Sonic Brew, dropped in 1999.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Other Projects</strong></h2>



<p>Ozzy Osbourne and Black Label Society are Wylde’s main gigs. But the great axeman also found time to form a Black Sabbath covers band, <strong><em>Zakk Sabbath</em></strong>, as well as two solo albums with 1996’s <strong><em>Book of Shadows</em></strong> and 2016’s <strong><em>Book of Shadows II</em></strong>.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Acting Career</strong></h2>



<p>Zakk Wylde isn’t just a killer guitar, the man is also an accomplished actor too. Wylde has earned himself parts in movies, TV shows, and cartoons, including Mark Wahlberg’s <strong><em>Rock Star, Aqua Teen Hunger Force, Californication, Angel, and Bones</em></strong>. In the latter, Wylde played Bones’ uncle, Jed.</p>

<p>Zakk Wylde is a very interesting guy. <h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Zakk Wylde Guitars: All The Model He’s Used Throughout His Career</strong></h2>



<p>Zakk Wylde’s most iconic guitar is, of course, his <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/gibson-les-paul-custom-vs-gibson-les-paul-standard/" data-type="post" data-id="2454" data-lasso-id="1570">Gibson Les Paul Custom</a></strong>, dubbed The Grail. If you’ve ever seen Zakk play, chances are he has The Grail strapped around his neck. You’ll know it&#8217;s The Grail because of its black and white bullseye paintwork.</p>



<p>Here’s a quote from Wylde about how he came to own The Grail (AKA his first Gibson Les Paul Custom):</p>
