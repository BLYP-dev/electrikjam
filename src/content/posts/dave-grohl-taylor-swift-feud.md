<p><strong>Dave Grohl and Taylor Swift are involved in a feud and are now actively slinging jibes at one another. There&#8217;s been death threats and a whole host of social media weirdness&#8230;</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-dots"/>



<p>Dave Grohl and Taylor Swift aren’t people like you and I, they’re extraordinarily famous, travel with entourages, and carry NDAs with them at all times. </p>



<p>Both are considered to be &#8220;nice&#8221; people, but even good dogs do bad things, and – thanks to the never-ending barf-train that social media is nowadays – Grohl and Swift are reportedly involved in something of a feud. </p>



<p>How? Why? It all started when Grohl’s daughter committed what is now considered the ultimate sin in our ultra-progressive, forward-thinking society: <strong>she spoke ill of Taylor Swift. </strong></p>



<p>From there the beef quickly escalated, culminating in death threats (no, I’m being serious), weird claims being made about AI porn, and snide remarks made in front of stadium-sized crowds. </p>



<p>Let&#8217;s unpack this shit-show, shall we? </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How The &#8220;Grohl x Swift Feud&#8221; Started</strong></h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image alignwide size-full"><img decoding="async" width="1020" height="574" src="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Taylor-Swift-news-Taylor-Swift-performs-with-Travis-Kelce-live-1.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-15490" title="" srcset="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Taylor-Swift-news-Taylor-Swift-performs-with-Travis-Kelce-live-1.jpg 1020w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Taylor-Swift-news-Taylor-Swift-performs-with-Travis-Kelce-live-1-600x338.jpg 600w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Taylor-Swift-news-Taylor-Swift-performs-with-Travis-Kelce-live-1-300x169.jpg 300w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Taylor-Swift-news-Taylor-Swift-performs-with-Travis-Kelce-live-1-768x432.jpg 768w" sizes="(max-width: 1020px) 100vw, 1020px" /></figure>



<p>The spark that ignited this fire came from an unlikely source – Grohl&#8217;s teenage daughter, Violet. </p>



<p>In January 2024, young Violet made some controversial comments about Swift&#8217;s carbon footprint, questioning why the pop star couldn&#8217;t <strong>&#8220;just drive like everyone else.&#8221; </strong></p>



<p>Swift&#8217;s notoriously passionate fanbase, the &#8220;Swifties,&#8221; responded in a way that would have brought a smile to Charles Manson&#8217;s face, pouring all the hate and scorn they could muster towards Ms. Grohl. </p>



<p>Violet must have not got the memo: you don&#8217;t say anything bad about Taylor Swift. It&#8217;s like the Beyonce thing from a few years ago. Social media&#8217;s feudalistic nature requires a queen and for better or worse it is now Taylor Swift.  </p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>How The Feud&#8217;s Going</strong>&#8230;</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image alignwide size-full"><img decoding="async" width="467" height="266" src="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/download.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-15491" title="" srcset="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/download.jpg 467w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/download-300x171.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 467px) 100vw, 467px" /></figure>



<p>Fast forward to June 2024, and it seems Papa Grohl decided to step into the ring. </p>



<p>At a Foo Fighters gig in London, Grohl took a not-so-subtle jab at Swift, implying that unlike his band&#8217;s &#8220;Errors Tour,&#8221; Swift&#8217;s &#8220;Eras Tour&#8221; might not be entirely live. &#8220;That&#8217;s because we actually play live,&#8221; Grohl quipped, sending the crowd into a frenzy and the internet into meltdown.</p>



<p>But Swift, never one to back down from a challenge, seemed to clap back the very next day. </p>



<p>During her London show, she pointedly praised her band for &#8220;playing live for you for 3.5 hours tonight.&#8221; Talk about a mic drop moment!</p>



<p>It&#8217;s a far cry from 2015 when Grohl jokingly declared himself &#8220;obsessed&#8221; with Swift after they both performed at a BBC event. Oh, how the tables have turned!</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Social Media Strikes Again </strong></h2>



<p>What started as a teenager&#8217;s tweet has escalated into a full-blown feud between two musical titans. </p>
