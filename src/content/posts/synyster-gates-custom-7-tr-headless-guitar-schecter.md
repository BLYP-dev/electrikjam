<p>The wait is over for fans of Avenged Sevenfold and Synyster Gates, as Schecter has officially launched the much-anticipated <strong>Synyster Gates Custom-7 TR Headless</strong> guitar. </p>



<p>This seven-string stunner, available in both right- and left-handed configurations, has been making waves on social media for months, and now we finally have the full specs. Spoiler alert: <strong>it’s every bit as jaw-dropping as we hoped.</strong></p>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="htoc-the-synyster-gates-custom-7-tr-headless-is-here-and-it-is-glorious"><strong>The Synyster Gates Custom-7 TR Headless Is Here And It Is Glorious&#8230;</strong></h3>



<figure class="wp-block-image alignwide size-large has-custom-border"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Synyster-Gates-Custom-7-TR-Headless-1024x683.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-16782" style="border-radius:20px" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Synyster-Gates-Custom-7-TR-Headless-1024x683.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Synyster-Gates-Custom-7-TR-Headless-600x400.jpg 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Synyster-Gates-Custom-7-TR-Headless-300x200.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Synyster-Gates-Custom-7-TR-Headless-768x512.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Synyster-Gates-Custom-7-TR-Headless.jpg 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>Schecter and Synyster Gates have had a long-standing partnership, producing a range of signature models that cater to the shredder’s need for speed and precision. </p>



<p>However, the Custom-7 TR Headless is something entirely different. It combines modern headless guitar design with Gates’ unmistakable flair, resulting in a guitar that’s as innovative as it is iconic.</p>



<p></div>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="htoc-let-s-talk-specs"><strong>Let’s Talk Specs</strong></h3>



<p>The Custom-7 TR Headless is built for performance, with a chambered mahogany body featuring a maple center. </p>



<p>This design, paired with Schecter’s <strong>Deep Insert Joint</strong> and <strong>Ultra Access neck construction</strong>, ensures effortless playability across the fretboard—a must for Synyster Gates’ intricate solos.</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li id="htoc-neck-a-maple-ultra-thin-c-profile-neck-reinforced-with-dual-carbon-fiber-rods-for-maximum-stability"><strong>Neck</strong>: A maple Ultra Thin C-profile neck reinforced with dual carbon fiber rods for maximum stability.</li>



<li id="htoc-fretboard-ebony-with-24-x-jumbo-gold-frets-and-luminlay-side-dots-for-visibility-in-low-light"><strong>Fretboard</strong>: Ebony with 24 X-Jumbo gold frets and Luminlay side dots for visibility in low light.</li>



<li id="htoc-pickups-schecter-usa-synyster-gates-signature-humbuckers-complete-with-push-pull-coil-splitting-for-tonal-versatility"><strong>Pickups</strong>: Schecter USA Synyster Gates Signature Humbuckers, complete with push-pull coil-splitting for tonal versatility.</li>



<li id="htoc-electronics-master-volume-and-master-tone-controls-plus-a-three-way-toggle-switch"><strong>Electronics</strong>: Master Volume and Master Tone controls, plus a three-way toggle switch.</li>
</ul>



<p></div>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="htoc-hardware-and-signature-touches"><strong>Hardware and Signature Touches</strong></h3>



<p>A standout feature is the <strong>Hipshot 7-String Headless bridge</strong>, complete with a whammy bar and bridge tuners, delivering tuning stability and smooth vibrato action. </p>



<p>Other hardware highlights include a <strong>Graph Tech XL Black Tusq nut</strong>, gold metal dome controls, and gold accents throughout.</p>



<p>Let’s not forget the aesthetic touches that scream Synyster Gates—literally. The guitar boasts a striking <strong>Oak Green Metallic finish</strong>, a gold ‘Syn S’ inlay at the 12th fret, and custom gold cavity covers featuring Gates’ signature branding. It’s bold, sleek, and unapologetically flashy.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="htoc-lefties-rejoice"><strong>Lefties, Rejoice!</strong></h3>



<p>In a move that’s sure to delight left-handed players, Schecter has made this model available in a lefty version right out of the gate. While lefty players are often left waiting or left out, this time they’re getting the rockstar treatment.</p>



<p></div>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>
