<p><strong>Josh Homme. The man, the myth, the legend. Here&#8217;s all the bands and projects Josh Homme has been involved in over the years&#8230;</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-dots"/>



<p>Josh Homme invented desert rock, was instrumental in making stoner rock a thing, popularised down-tuned rock and roll, and is the pride and joy of red-headed males all over the planet. As musicians go, Homme is one of the greats of the modern era – from KYUSS to QOTSA, he is rarely puts a foot wrong (save for that one time, when his foot did do something wrong).</p>



<p>From killer, iconic riffs to writing some of the best music of the last two decades, The Big J has done it all. But if you&#8217;re just getting started with Queens, you&#8217;re in for a treat – <strong><a href="/queens-of-the-stone-age-cancel-entire-european-tour/" data-type="post" data-id="15569" data-lasso-id="267716">Josh Homme</a></strong> has been in LOADS of other bands. And you&#8217;re about to meet (and) hear them all&#8230; </p>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="Kyuss"><strong>Kyuss</strong></h3>



<p>Before he was the king of Queens, Homme cut his teeth in the sweltering crucible of Palm Desert, California. </p>

<p>Kyuss, formed when Homme was barely old enough to drive, became the torchbearers of the &#8220;desert rock&#8221; sound. Their thick, fuzzed-out riffs and hypnotic grooves laid the groundwork for stoner rock as we know it.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="Queens_of_the_Stone_Age"><strong>Queens of the Stone Age</strong></h3>



<p>QOTSA is where Homme truly came into his own as a frontman and songwriter. </p>

<p>From the self-titled debut to the Grammy-nominated &#8220;&#8230;Like Clockwork,&#8221; Queens has been the primary vehicle for Homme&#8217;s distinctive blend of heavy riffs, pop hooks, and sardonic wit.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="Eagles_of_Death_Metal"><strong>Eagles of Death Metal</strong></h3>



<p>Don&#8217;t let the name fool you – there&#8217;s nary a death metal growl to be found here. </p>



<figure class="wp-block-embed is-type-video is-provider-youtube wp-block-embed-youtube wp-embed-aspect-16-9 wp-has-aspect-ratio"><div class="wp-block-embed__wrapper">

<p>With childhood friend Jesse Hughes at the helm and Homme often behind the drum kit, EODM serves up a platter of swaggering, tongue-in-cheek rock &#8216;n&#8217; roll.</p>

<p>When you team up with Dave Grohl and John Paul Jones, expectations are bound to be sky-high. </p>

<p>Them Crooked Vultures didn&#8217;t disappoint, delivering a slab of hard-rocking, prog-tinged excellence that left fans clamoring for more.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="The_Collaborator:_Hommes_Guest_Appearances"><strong>The Collaborator: Homme&#8217;s Guest Appearances</strong></h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image alignwide size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Them-Crooked-Vultures-Reunion-Its-All-On-Dave-Grohl-Apparently…-1024x683.png" alt="" class="wp-image-15495" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Them-Crooked-Vultures-Reunion-Its-All-On-Dave-Grohl-Apparently…-1024x683.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Them-Crooked-Vultures-Reunion-Its-All-On-Dave-Grohl-Apparently…-600x400.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Them-Crooked-Vultures-Reunion-Its-All-On-Dave-Grohl-Apparently…-300x200.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Them-Crooked-Vultures-Reunion-Its-All-On-Dave-Grohl-Apparently…-768x512.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Them-Crooked-Vultures-Reunion-Its-All-On-Dave-Grohl-Apparently….png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>From Arctic Monkeys to Iggy Pop, Homme&#8217;s guitar work and production skills have graced albums by some of rock&#8217;s biggest names. His collaboration with Iggy on &#8220;Post Pop Depression&#8221; stands as a particular high water mark.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="Mondo_Generator"><strong>Mondo Generator</strong></h3>



<p>While not strictly a Homme project, his contributions to Nick Oliveri&#8217;s Mondo Generator showcase the enduring musical bond between these two desert rock veterans.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="The_Rekords_Rekords_Label"><strong>The Rekords Rekords Label</strong></h3>



<p>Homme&#8217;s record label has been a platform for up-and-coming artists and side projects, further cementing his role as a tastemaker in the rock world.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="Producer_Credits"><strong>Producer Credits</strong></h2>



<p>Homme&#8217;s talents extend beyond performing, with an impressive list of production credits to his name. </p>



<p>Some notable examples include:</p>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li>Arctic Monkeys &#8211; &#8220;Humbug&#8221; (2009)</li>



<li>Iggy Pop &#8211; &#8220;Post Pop Depression&#8221; (2016)</li>



<li>The Desert Sessions &#8211; Volumes 9 &amp; 10 (2003) and 11 &amp; 12 (2019)</li>



<li>Royal Blood &#8211; &#8220;Royal Blood&#8221; (2014) &#8211; Co-producer on two tracks</li>



<li>CKY &#8211; &#8220;Volume 1&#8221; (1999) &#8211; Co-producer</li>



<li>Mark Lanegan &#8211; &#8220;Bubblegum&#8221; (2004) &#8211; Co-producer</li>



<li>Mastodon &#8211; &#8220;Crack the Skye&#8221; (2009) &#8211; Additional production</li>



<li>Foo Fighters &#8211; &#8220;Concrete and Gold&#8221; (2017) &#8211; Guest appearance and production on one track</li>
</ol>



<p></div>



<p>Homme&#8217;s production style often brings out the raw, unfiltered essence of the artists he works with, adding his signature touch without overshadowing the original vision.</p>



<p>As the man himself once said, <strong>&#8220;I think you should make something that you would want to hear.&#8221;</strong> And for decades now, that&#8217;s exactly what he&#8217;s been doing – what a f**king legend. </p>
