<h2 class="wp-block-heading">How Does A Wah Pedal Work?</h2>



<p><strong>The Wah Pedal works on a rocker switch. When you pull the switch back, you change the frequency output of the instrument. With guitar, the Wah acts like a tone knob. When you pull the pedal backwards (cocked) it acts as a low pass filter. Low frequencies pass through, where highs are blocked. The opposite happens when you push the pedal forward, taking out the low frequency. Rocking it back and forth &#8220;sweeps&#8221; the frequency. </strong></p>



<p></div>
