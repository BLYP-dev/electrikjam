<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="What_Guitar_Does_Tony_Iommi_Use?">What Guitar Does Tony Iommi Use?</h2>



<p>Tony Iommi is best known as the founding guitarist of the pioneering heavy metal band Black Sabbath. Iommi is widely regarded as one of the most influential guitarists in rock and metal history, known for his dark, heavy riffs and innovative solos.</p>

<p><strong>Tony</strong> <strong>Iommi&#8217;s guitar of choice throughout his career has been the Gibson SG. His main guitar is a 1964 Gibson SG Special nicknamed &#8220;Monkey.&#8221; </strong></p>



<p><strong>This SG Special features a cherry red finish, two Gibson P90 pickups, and a stopbar tailpiece. Iommi has used this guitar to record many of Black Sabbath&#8217;s classic albums including Paranoid, Master of Reality, and Vol 4.</strong></p>

<p>The Gibson SG stands out in the guitar world, not just for its distinctive looks but for its unique design that caters to players with specific needs. </p>



<p>Take the legendary Tony Iommi, for instance. His story with the SG is not just about preference, but necessity. </p>



<p>After a <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/doom-metal-beginners-guide/" data-type="post" data-id="1042" data-lasso-id="254688">tragic industrial accident </a></strong>that cost him the tips of two fingers, Iommi faced a daunting challenge in playing the guitar. The SG, with its thinner, sculpted body, was a game-changer for him. It&#8217;s lighter, reducing the strain on his fretting hand, which is crucial for someone adapting to such an injury.</p>



<p>But it&#8217;s not just the weight that makes the SG a perfect match for Iommi. The design of the neck plays a pivotal role. Known for its comfortable and fast neck, the SG complements Iommi&#8217;s heavy and swift playing style. </p>



<p>The damn thing was made for shredding, with a neck that invites quick finger movements, making it easier for Iommi to create his signature riffs.</p>



<p>The dual <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/humbuckers-vs-single-coil-pickups/" data-type="post" data-id="2609" data-lasso-id="254689">humbucker pickups</a></strong> are another key feature. They&#8217;re designed to cancel out interference and noise, providing a cleaner, richer sound. </p>



<p>For a genre like heavy metal, where distortion is a staple, these pickups are a powerhouse, offering the crunch and sustain that Iommi&#8217;s music demands.</p>



<p>Iommi&#8217;s relationship with the SG goes beyond functionality. It&#8217;s personal. Over the years, he&#8217;s played various SG models, including custom ones adorned with cross inlays that reflect his iconic status in the music world. </p>



<p>Yet, it&#8217;s his 1964 SG Special that remains his steadfast companion, both on stage and in the studio. And it looks and sounds as good today as it did more than 50 years ago. </p>
