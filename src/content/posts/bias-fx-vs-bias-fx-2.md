<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="strong-bias-fx-what-it-is-amp-why-it-matters-strong"><strong>BIAS FX: What It Is &amp; Why It Matters…</strong></h2>



<p>Tube amps are insanely expensive. Even all-in-one setups, iconic ones like the Fender Reverb Deluxe will set you back almost a couple of grand.</p>



<p>Most beginner guitarists <strong>either do not want and/or cannot</strong> spend this much money on a decent amp. Plus, when you’re just getting started, you’ll want to save all your money for a decent guitar most likely.</p>



<p>If you go the amp route, you’ll need things like pedals and effects pedals to really create a unique tone. And pedals, again, are not cheap. </p>



<p>On top of this, you then have to learn how to use them and set up your pedalboard.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-full"><img decoding="async" width="600" height="600" src="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/BIAS-FX-2-review.webp" alt="" class="wp-image-10654" title="" srcset="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/BIAS-FX-2-review.webp 600w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/BIAS-FX-2-review-300x300.webp 300w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/BIAS-FX-2-review-100x100.webp 100w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/BIAS-FX-2-review-150x150.webp 150w" sizes="(max-width: 600px) 100vw, 600px" /></figure>



<p>Or, you could ditch all those costs and headaches and use something like BIAS FX, a digital FX and amp simulator.</p>



<p>BIAS FX gives you access to literally every pedal, amp, microphone, and effect in existence on your computer which, if you’re just starting out or cannot afford to spend $3000 to $4000 on a rig, is infinitely more practical.</p>



<p>As noted inside <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/what-is-bias-fx-2-guide/" data-type="post" data-id="1728" data-lasso-id="216006">our review of BIAS FX 2</a></strong>, this software is pretty mind-blowing. If you’ve never used or heard of something like this before, it will blow your mind.</p>



<p>The attention to detail that has gone into designing and making BIAS FX and BIAS FX 2 is otherworldly. From the UX design to its partnership with Celestion for the speakers it uses, the scope of what you can do with this software is essentially limitless.</p>



<p>Positive Grid, the brains behind BIAS FX, now offers two versions of its guitar rig software in the form of BIAS FX and BIAS FX 2. But what are the main differences between BIAS FX and BIAS FZ 2?</p>



<p>Let’s dig in and find out…</p>

<p>One of the most significant differences between BIAS FX and BIAS FX 2 is their DSP engines. <h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="improved-amp-tones">Improved Amp Tones</h3>



<p>Another area where BIAS FX 2 improves upon its predecessor is in its amp tones. </p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="way-more-effects-pedals-amps-amp-racks">Way More Effects, Pedals, Amps &amp; Racks</h3>



<p>BIAS FX 2 also offers more effects than its predecessor. With <strong>over 100 new effects pedals, racks, and amps</strong>, there are plenty of options to choose from when creating your perfect tone.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="improved-tone-cloud-integration">Improved ToneCloud Integration</h3>



<p>ToneCloud is Positive Grid&#8217;s online community where users can share their custom presets and settings with others.</p>



<p>While both versions of BIAS FX offer ToneCloud integration, BIAS FX 2 takes it a step further by allowing users to upload and <strong>download entire rigs, including amps, pedals, and cabinets</strong>.</p>

<p>Finally, while both versions of BIAS FX feature intuitive user interfaces that make it easy to create custom tones, <strong>BIAS FX 2 has an updated interface that looks even better</strong> than its predecessor.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="strong-is-bias-fx-2-worth-it-strong"><strong>Is BIAS FX 2 Worth It?</strong></h2>



<p>BIAS FX was, obviously, the first iteration of Positive Grid’s guitar FX software. It was great when it came out, scoring tens of thousands of downloads. </p>



<p>But BIAS FX 2 is a different beast altogether, packing in more of everything –&nbsp;more pedals, more effects, and improved sound quality, thanks to its completely redesigned DSP.</p>



<p>If you’re looking to invest in a digital guitar FC software, BIAS FX 2 is 100% the one you want to be looking at. </p>



<p>The <strong>difference between the two is like night and day</strong>, especially where it really counts (how it actually sounds and what you can do with FX and pedals).</p>



<p>In BIAS FX 2, you also get access to the Celestion’s full array of speakers too, so you can further customize the sound of your rig by swapping out different speakers to see how the change the tone.</p>



<p>Add in stuff like the ability to do things like microphone placement and switch out different types of microphones and you’re looking at one of the most comprehensive guitar FX and tone packages on the market right now.</p>



<p>We’re HUGE fans of BIAS FX 2 here at ELECTRIKJAM, it is something we recommend to all of our readers. For crafting guitar tone and building out unique sounds, nothing else comes even remotely close in our minds.</p>



<p>The software is easy to use, it sounds incredible, and it gives you an incredible overview of what is possible with gear if budget is no concern. You need to keep in mind that physically owning all of the gear contained inside BIAS FX 2 would cost you millions of dollars.</p>
