<p>Fender has officially unveiled a faithful replica of Tom Morello&#8217;s legendary &#8220;Arm The Homeless&#8221; guitar, the heavily modified six-string that fueled the groundbreaking sound of Rage Against the Machine and Audioslave.</p>



<p>After 30 years as the Rage Against the Machine firebrand&#8217;s primary instrument, the unique, custom-Frankensteined guitar has been meticulously re-created by Fender. However, the guitar’s market positioning is already sparking controversy among fans.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The &#8220;Misfit Toy&#8221; Recreated</h2>

<p>The original &#8220;Arm the Homeless&#8221; was an infamous custom order that Morello initially considered the &#8220;worst guitar I’d ever played.&#8221; </p>



<p>Through years of swapping parts—from a random neck found in a used bin to an array of non-standard electronics, it became the tool that forced Morello to &#8220;stop whining, and I&#8217;m going to start creating.&#8221;</p>



<p>Fender and Morello worked through &#8220;20-25 iterations&#8221; to nail the feel of the unique neck and replicate the instrument&#8217;s idiosyncratic features:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Custom Electronics: An active EMG 85 bridge humbucker paired with an EMG H single-coil in the neck—a configuration that provided the &#8220;springiness&#8221; for his distinctive riffs.</li>



<li>The Killswitch: A durable 3-way toggle switch engineered to withstand the brutal on/off abuse that creates his signature stuttering, rhythmic effects.</li>



<li>Locking Tremolo: A Gotoh GE1996T double-locking tremolo system, faithfully replicating the unit whose &#8220;looseness&#8221; Morello used to create his &#8220;helicopter sound&#8221; and other noise effects.</li>



<li>Iconic Art: The replica features the hand-scrawled &#8220;Arm the Homeless&#8221; slogan, the cartoon hippos, and the uncut strings at the hockey-stick headstock. Morello confirmed he’s been &#8220;stealth-playing&#8221; the final prototype on tour, and zero percent of the audience noticed.</li>
</ul>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>It&#8217;s A MIM Priced Like A Custom Shop</strong></h2>



<p>While the replica is a technical marvel, its retail price point of approximately $1,699 USD (£1,699 GBP) is raising eyebrows because the instrument is Made In Mexico (MIM).</p>



<p>Me? I&#8217;d take a <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/fender-american-ultra-ii-hss-stratocaster-reviews-gods-guitar/" data-type="post" data-id="18174">Fender American Ultra 2 Stratocaster</a> </strong>over one of these every day of the week and twice on a Sunday. </p>



<p>This price places the Tom Morello signature model alongside, and in some cases above, entry-level Fender Made In America (MIA) instruments. Critics argue that this represents an &#8220;American price tag&#8221; for a guitar built in the Ensenada, Mexico factory.</p>



<p>Morello, however, rejected the idea of a high-end, American-made Custom Shop version, stating he wanted fans to create their own relic’ing: </p>



<blockquote class="wp-block-quote is-layout-flow wp-block-quote-is-layout-flow">
<p>&#8220;No, I didn&#8217;t. Let the relic’ing and road wear be your own. Here it is. Now it&#8217;s up to you to let your dog bite it.&#8221;</p>
</blockquote>



<p>Adding an altruistic dimension, a portion of the proceeds from the guitar&#8217;s sale will be donated to charitable organizations, including Covenant House and LA&#8217;s Midnight Mission shelter, reflecting the political message scrawled on the guitar&#8217;s body.</p>
