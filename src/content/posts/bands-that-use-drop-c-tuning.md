<p><strong>Drop C tuning sounds heavy –&nbsp;heavy as heck. It also makes playing power chords nice and easy too. Here’s 10 awesome bands that use Drop C tuning</strong>&#8230;</p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator"/>



<p>Metal is all about tone. Getting the heaviest sound possible doesn’t always require that you tune down. Opeth and The Dillinger Escape Plan both play in <strong><a href="/guitar-tuning/" data-lasso-id="1708">standard tuning</a></strong> and they’re heavier than a ton of bricks.</p>



<p>And this is ALL down to their <strong><a class="rank-math-link" href="/chord-voicing-extensions-for-metal/" data-lasso-id="778">masterful use of chord voicing and extensions. </a></strong></p>



<p>But for that lovely-sounding, low-end chug we all love so much, you’ll want to tune down a few steps from standard. Drop D is a very popular choice, of course, and is used by <a data-lasso-id="779" href="/adam-jones-epiphone-1979-les-paul-custom-release-date-specs/">bands like TOOL</a> and Lamb of God.</p>



<p>But what if you want to go even lowlier? Well, the next stop of the down-tuning train from <a data-lasso-id="780" href="/d-standard-tuning-guide-how-to/">Drop D</a> is, of course, Drop C. And <a data-lasso-id="781" href="/the-3-best-strings-for-drop-c-tuning/">Drop C tuning</a> is just about as low as you can go on a standard, six-string guitar without the strings getting too wobbly.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Drop C Tuning: How To Do It</strong></h2>



<p>With Drop C tuning, <strong>your low string is dropped two whole steps down</strong> and the rest of the strings one full step down. <strong>The end result should look like this: CGCFAD</strong> –&nbsp;with CGC being the three thickest strings.</p>



<p>Once you have that dialed in, you can play power chords the same as you do in drop d tuning – just place your finger across the top two or three strings to make a power chord anywhere on the neck. No barre chords are necessary.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1) High on Fire / Sleep</strong></h2>



<p><a href="/matt-pike-dopesmoker-sleep/" data-lasso-id="784">Matt Pike</a> is one of the most influential doom/sludge players of the last 20 years. He doesn’t always use Drop C tuning, <a href="/matt-pike-guitar-guide-a-complete-list-of-what-he-uses/" data-lasso-id="785">most Sleep is in C Standard</a>, but Pike does use Drop C a lot in High on Fire.</p>



<p>And the reason?</p>



<p>High on Fire is fast. The riffs are more punk than blues and this is where a tuning like Drop C comes into its own. With Drop C, you can play power chords with a single finger. And this makes shredding through a riff at break-neck speeds a lot easier.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Drop C Tuning FAQs</strong></h3>


<div id="rank-math-faq" class="rank-math-block">
<div class="rank-math-list ">
<div id="faq-question-1612785571634" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">How Do I Tune My Guitar To Drop C?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>Tuning to drop C is easy. First, most people need to up their string gauge a little. Usually 11-52 is a good place to start for a 25.5 scale inch guitar.</p>
<p>Start by turning your guitar a full step down on all the strings to D standard. This means your lowest and highest string will be D. With all strings in between being one full step down.</p>
<p>Going from high to low:</p>
<p><strong>D<br />A<br />F<br />C<br />G<br />D</strong></p>
<p>To get to drop C, tune down your lowest string from D to C.</p>

</div>
</div>
<div id="faq-question-1612785703348" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">Will Drop C Tuning Damage My Guitar?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>Down tuning your guitar to drop c is not going to damage your guitar. Most six string guitars, providing that you’re using the right string gauge, will be able to handle drop C with ease. If your guitar is struggling, or the bottom string is too wobbly, it means you need to use heavier gauge strings.</p>

</div>
</div>
<div id="faq-question-1612785715941" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">What Bands Use Drop C Tuning?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>Loads of metal and sludge bands use Drop C tuning like <strong>Converge, Sleep, High on Fire, Deftones, Mastodon, The Sword, August Burns Red, Stoned Jesus, and Ministry</strong> to name just a few&#8230;</p>

</div>
</div>
<div id="faq-question-1612785814239" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">What String Gauge is Best For Drop C?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>The best string gauge for drop C, based on what most players use, and my own personal experience would be <strong>11-52</strong>. This will ensure your strings, even when heavily down-tuned will still hold plenty of tension which is exactly what you want while your sliding around the neck.</p>
