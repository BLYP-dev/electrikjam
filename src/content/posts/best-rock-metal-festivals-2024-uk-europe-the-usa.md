<p><strong>Here&#8217;s a quick overview of the all the best rock and metal festivals happening in the UK, Europe, and the USA during 2024&#8230;</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-dots"/>



<p>Despite the ups and downs in the music industry, rock and metal fans can always rely on an incredible festival scene to get their fix of thunderous riffs and headbanging. </p>



<p>2024 promises another stellar year of top events celebrating hard rock, metal, punk, alternative, and everything loud. Let&#8217;s explore some of the can&#8217;t-miss fests in the UK, USA, and around the world.</p>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="Best_Rock_and_Metal_Festivals_UK_amp;_Europe_">Best Rock and Metal Festivals UK &amp; Europe </h2>



<p>The UK boasts some of the best rock and metal festivals in Europe, pleasing fans seeking either massive events or intimate gatherings.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="ELECTRIKJAMs_Pick_of_The_Litter:">ELECTRIKJAM&#8217;s Pick of The Litter:</h3>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Derbyshire&#8217;s Bloodstock Open Air</strong> is the ultimate destination for metal enthusiasts, featuring a mix of emerging talents and well-established acts like Opeth, Architects, and many others, all ready to rock out with the horns-up crowd.</li>



<li>Mid-August brings Bristol&#8217;s <strong>ArcTanGent Festival</strong> to life, drawing fans of math-rock, post-rock, and prog music genres. With favorites like And So I Watch You From Afar already on the lineup, it&#8217;s a must-attend event for aficionados of these distinctive musical styles.</li>



<li>In June, France&#8217;s <strong>Hellfest</strong> lives up to its name, delivering a heavy dose of metal mayhem with headlining acts such as Foo Fighters and Queens of the Stone Age, set to ignite the passions of tens of thousands of fervent fans.</li>
</ul>



<p></div>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">From Bloodstock To Hellfest</h2>



<p>Kicking off 2024 is <strong>Winter&#8217;s End</strong> in Wales this February. With 21 bands gracing a single stage, highlights include British rockers Massive Wagons, Those Damn Crows, and Stone Broken.</p>



<p><strong>Bearded Theory</strong> in Derbyshire this May offers musical diversity with alt-rockers Jane&#8217;s Addiction and indie darlings Future Islands amid the lineup. Beyond the music, it provides a community vibe and has won &#8220;Best Family Festival&#8221; five years running.</p>



<p><strong>Slam Dunk Festival</strong> across Leeds, Hatfield and Birmingham in late May aims to be the UK&#8217;s premier rock event. Pop-punk and emo acts like You Me At Six and The All American Rejects take top billing.</p>



<p>The hallowed grounds of <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/download-2024-lineup/" data-type="post" data-id="13529" data-lasso-id="259745">Download Festival at Donington Park come alive in June</a></strong>. Metal titans Avenged Sevenfold and Queens of the Stone Age lead a heavyweight lineup. Pantera&#8217;s headlining slot will be their first UK show in over 20 years!</p>



<p>Details are still emerging for <strong>Glastonbury Festival</strong> in late June, but expect a high-caliber rock presence alongside the iconic pop acts.</p>



<p><strong>Hellfest</strong> in France lives up to its name in June, promising the biggest names in metal like Foo Fighters and Queens of the Stone Age for tens of thousands of rabid fans.</p>



<p>Meanwhile, 2000trees Festival in Gloucestershire keeps things intimate, focusing on underground and up-and-coming British guitar bands throughout its mid-July run.</p>



<p>Wales hosts another banger with July&#8217;s <strong>Steelhouse Festival</strong>, introducing a &#8220;Berniefest&#8221; day to honor the legendary Bernie Marsden and featuring greats like King King and Living Colour.</p>



<p>Derbyshire&#8217;s <strong>Bloodstock Open Air</strong> in August showcases new and established metal with Opeth, Architects and more for the horns-up crowd.</p>



<p>Math-rock, post-rock and prog fans flock to <strong>ArcTanGent Festival</strong> in Bristol in mid-August, with genre faves And So I Watch You From Afar already confirmed.</p>



<p>Closing the UK summer in late August are the twin <strong>Reading and Leeds Festivals</strong>. With huge rock names alongside pop headliners like Lana Del Rey, these festivals have something for everyone.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="Best_Rock_and_Metal_Festivals_USA">Best Rock and Metal Festivals USA</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/DOWNLOAD-2024-1024x683.png" alt="DOWNLOAD 2024 lineup" class="wp-image-13531" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/DOWNLOAD-2024-1024x683.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/11/DOWNLOAD-2024-600x400.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/11/DOWNLOAD-2024-300x200.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/11/DOWNLOAD-2024-768x512.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/11/DOWNLOAD-2024.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>You can count on the United States to deliver some of the biggest and best rock and metal fests on Earth. Let&#8217;s dive into the must-see events across America in 2024.</p>



<p>Taking over Daytona Beach, Florida in early May, <strong>Welcome to Rockville</strong> promises to be the East Coast&#8217;s biggest rock bash. Metallica and Nine Inch Nails are already locked as headliners.</p>



<p>Out west, California&#8217;s <strong>Aftershock Festival</strong> in October is the West Coast&#8217;s premier rock/metal event. The lineup is already massive with Slipknot, Kiss, and Judas Priest.</p>



<p><strong>Louder Than Life</strong> brings the noise to Louisville, Kentucky in late September. Recently awarded &#8220;Festival of the Year,&#8221; it&#8217;s one of the country&#8217;s most prestigious metal fests.</p>



<p>For an intimate open-air metal experience, head to <strong>Full Terror Assault</strong> in Illinois in September. Fans can camp and bring their own booze while rocking to over 40 bands.</p>



<p>Oklahoma&#8217;s <strong>Rocklahoma</strong> takes over Memorial Day weekend with three days of nonstop rock and camping under the stars.</p>



<p><strong>Maryland Deathfest</strong> in Baltimore every May is a pilgrimage for metalheads, bringing the heaviest bands from across the globe. The epic lineup will be announced soon.</p>



<p><strong>Rock Fest</strong> in Cadott, Wisconsin is a long-running July 4th tradition, as generations of rock fans have packed its permanent venue to see legends grace the stage.</p>



<p>What happens when you mix Las Vegas with metal? <strong>Psycho Las Vegas</strong> in August 2024 will show you, taking over Mandalay Bay for a headbanging weekend.</p>



<p><strong>ProgPower USA</strong> in Atlanta is a prog and power metal fan&#8217;s dream, bringing international talent stateside every September.</p>



<p>Can&#8217;t decide on a festival? How about all of them? <strong>70000 Tons of Metal</strong> sets sail from Miami in January 2024, hitting two destinations with 60 bands onboard.</p>



<p><strong>Sonic Temple</strong> in Columbus, Ohio keeps the spirit of Rock On The Range alive in May 2024, promising massive production and crowds.</p>



<p>Other standouts include Iowa&#8217;s Knotfest, Furnace Fest in Alabama, Texas&#8217; Metal Threat Fest and more. There&#8217;s a rock festival for every state and taste!</p>
