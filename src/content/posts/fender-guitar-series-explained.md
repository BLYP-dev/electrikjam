<p><strong>Here&#8217;s literally everything you need to know about Fender&#8217;s guitar series: how they work, how they&#8217;re different, and which one is right for you&#8230;</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-dots"/>



<p>Fender makes a slew of iconic guitars – from the Strat to the Tele to the Jazzmaster. It also produces different versions of these models inside specific &#8220;series&#8221; across a range of price points. </p>



<p>From high-end, custom shop models to entry-level, intermediate models, there&#8217;s a Fender guitar series for every kind of player –&nbsp;even you! </p>



<p>It can be confusing, though –&nbsp;especially if you&#8217;re new to the world of Fender guitars. Don&#8217;t fret (pun intended). It&#8217;s actually pretty simple to understand once it is all laid out in plain English. </p>



<p>Here&#8217;s a complete breakdown of Fender&#8217;s current guitar series, what they all mean, how they&#8217;re different, and how the cost of each series breaks down into dollars and cents. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Which Model Fender Is Best For You? </strong></h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image alignwide size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-1024x683.png" alt="Fender-Player-Stratocaster-review" class="wp-image-10884" title="" srcset="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-1024x683.png 1024w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-600x400.png 600w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-300x200.png 300w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster-768x512.png 768w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Fender-Player-Stratocaster.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>I don&#8217;t know who you are, your budget, or the kind of music you want to play. All I can do is give you my opinion (based on 25 years&#8217; worth of playing live, in studios, and all over America). </p>



<p>For the vast majority of people that frequent this site –&nbsp;metalheads and aspiring rock guitarists –&nbsp;you’re going to be best off with something like a Fender Stratocaster or a Fender Telecaster.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Why? These guitars are essentially do-it-all electric guitars. They’re chameleons. They can change and adapt to pretty much any style of music –&nbsp;from jazz to folk to metal and prog.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Personally, I honestly do not think there is a better intermediate guitar series on the market now that it is better than <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/fender-player-ii-series/" data-type="post" data-id="15565" data-lasso-id="268996">Fender’s Player Series II</a></strong> –&nbsp;it’s so damn close to a custom shop model it’s crazy.&nbsp;</p>



<p>My advice? Get one of those. Nothing else on the market from Gibson or PRS or even Ibanez comes even remotely close right now for value for money, tone, and overall playability.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Of course, if you want or need something cheaper there is always the chug-monster itself, the <a data-lasso-id="268997" href="https://www.electrikjam.com/squier-contemporary-telecaster-review/"><strong>Squier Contemporary</strong> <strong>Telecaster</strong></a>. It’s around $400 and it is a metal machine with insanely hot pickups and a modern reimagining of the classic Tele design. </p>
