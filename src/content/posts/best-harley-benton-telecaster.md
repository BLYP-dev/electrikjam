<p>If you want a proper do-it-all guitar, there really isn’t a better style of electric guitar on the market than the humble Telecaster.</p>



<p>Lauded by many as the ultimate electric guitar, Fender’s Telecaster is still as popular today as it was 70 years ago, when it first hit the market, demonstrating the sheer, total genius of its creator, Leo Fender, who back in the early-1950s when the Tele first launched was laughed at for designing a guitar that looked like a paddle.</p>



<p>Well, Ol’ Leo had the last laugh, earning himself tens of millions of dollars from his iconic design which, in the space of just a few short years, went on to become one of the fastest and consistently best-selling electric guitars of all time.</p>



<p>Nowadays, you can get Tele-style guitars from a range of different guitar brands –&nbsp;from Charvel to Squier and, in the context of this article, <strong>Harley Benton</strong>.</p>



<p>You can pay anywhere from $5000 to less than $200 for a Tele guitar in today’s market but for those of you after an ultra-cheap but perfectly useable Tele in 2023 and beyond, Harley Benton is where you want to be looking.</p>



<p>The main benefit of going with a Harley Benton Telecaster over, say, one from Fender, Schecter, or Charvel is the cost –&nbsp;<strong>Harley Benton Telecasters are WAY cheaper</strong> with prices starting lower than $200 in most cases. </p>
