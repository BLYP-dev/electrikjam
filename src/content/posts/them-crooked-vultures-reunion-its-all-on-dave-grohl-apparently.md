<p>In the annals of rock history, few collaborations have generated as much excitement and intrigue as Them Crooked Vultures. </p>



<p>This supergroup, formed in 2009, brought together three titans of rock: <strong><a href="/queens-of-the-stone-age-guitar-tuning-a-guide/" data-type="post" data-id="5551" data-lasso-id="266532">Josh Homme of Queens of the Stone Age</a></strong>, <strong><a href="/dave-grohl-taylor-swift-feud/" data-type="post" data-id="15487" data-lasso-id="266533">Dave Grohl of Foo Fighters</a></strong> (and formerly Nirvana), and <strong><a href="/led-zeppelin-guitar-tunings-alternate-tunings/" data-type="post" data-id="3048" data-lasso-id="266534">John Paul Jones of Led Zeppelin</a></strong> fame.</p>



<p>Their union was a dream come true for rock enthusiasts, promising a sonic explosion that would shake the foundations of modern music.</p>



<div class="custom-snippet-div"></p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Birth of a Supergroup</strong></h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image alignwide size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Them-Crooked-Vultures-New-Album-1024x683.png" alt="Them Crooked Vultures New Album" class="wp-image-7264" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Them-Crooked-Vultures-New-Album-1024x683.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Them-Crooked-Vultures-New-Album-600x400.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Them-Crooked-Vultures-New-Album-300x200.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Them-Crooked-Vultures-New-Album-768x512.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Them-Crooked-Vultures-New-Album.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>The genesis of Them Crooked Vultures can be traced back to 2005 when Homme and Grohl first discussed the idea of collaborating with Jones. However, it wasn&#8217;t until 2009 that this dream became a reality. </p>



<p>The band&#8217;s formation was shrouded in secrecy, with cryptic messages and teasers fueling fan speculation for months before their official announcement.</p>



<p>Their self-titled debut album, released on November 17, 2009, was a tour de force that lived up to the hype. </p>



<p>The record showcased a perfect blend of each member&#8217;s distinctive style – Homme&#8217;s desert rock riffs, Grohl&#8217;s thunderous drumming, and Jones&#8217; virtuosic bass lines and keyboard work. </p>



<p>Tracks like &#8220;New Fang&#8221; and &#8220;Mind Eraser, No Chaser&#8221; quickly became fan favorites, earning critical acclaim and commercial success.</p>



<p>The album&#8217;s production was as noteworthy as its content. Recorded at Homme&#8217;s Pink Duck Studios in Burbank, California, the band used a plethora of vintage equipment to achieve their unique sound. </p>



<p>This included John Paul Jones&#8217; 1968 Fender Jazz Bass, which he had used on several classic Led Zeppelin recordings.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>A Brief but Memorable Run</strong></h2>



<p>Them Crooked Vultures embarked on a world tour to support their album, playing over 100 shows across North America, Europe, and Australia. </p>



<p>Their live performances were electrifying, often stretching beyond two hours and featuring extended jams that showcased the band&#8217;s improvisational prowess.</p>



<p>One of the most memorable moments of their tour occurred at the Royal Albert Hall in London, where they were joined on stage by Jimmy Page for a rendition of Led Zeppelin&#8217;s &#8220;Good Times Bad Times.&#8221; </p>



<p>This moment, bridging rock&#8217;s past and present, left an indelible mark on those fortunate enough to witness it.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>The Long Wait for More</strong></h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image alignwide size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/What-Pickups-Does-Josh-Homme-Use-1024x683.png" alt="What Pickups Does Josh Homme Use" class="wp-image-7071" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/What-Pickups-Does-Josh-Homme-Use-1024x683.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/03/What-Pickups-Does-Josh-Homme-Use-600x400.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/03/What-Pickups-Does-Josh-Homme-Use-300x200.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/03/What-Pickups-Does-Josh-Homme-Use-768x512.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/03/What-Pickups-Does-Josh-Homme-Use-1080x720.png 1080w, /wp-content/uploads/2022/03/What-Pickups-Does-Josh-Homme-Use.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>Despite the band&#8217;s success and the obvious chemistry between its members, fans have been left waiting for a follow-up album. </p>



<p>The group went on hiatus in 2010, with each member returning to their other projects. Rumors of a reunion have persisted over the years, fueled by occasional comments from the band members themselves.</p>



<p>The closest fans have come to a reunion was at the Taylor Hawkins tribute show on September 3, 2022. </p>



<p>This emotional performance saw the band come together to honor their late friend and collaborator, playing a Queens of the Stone Age cover, an Elton John cover, and their own track &#8220;Gunman.&#8221; The performance reignited hopes for a full-fledged reunion.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Hope for the Future?</strong></h2>



<p>In a recent Reddit AMA, Josh Homme addressed the possibility of a Them Crooked Vultures reunion. He stated that while there&#8217;s always a chance, it&#8217;s ultimately up to Dave Grohl to initiate. </p>



<p>Homme expressed his desire to reunite, praising the band&#8217;s unique sound and the joy he experienced during their time together.</p>



<p>Homme&#8217;s comments reveal the complexities of managing a supergroup. With each member juggling multiple projects and responsibilities, coordinating schedules and creative energies can be challenging. </p>



<p>Nevertheless, the door remains open, even if slightly.</p>
