<p><strong>How much does Cubase cost? It depends on which version you go with –&nbsp;there&#8217;s Elements, Artist, and Pro. And they&#8217;re all different. Here&#8217;s everything you need to know about pricing for all of them</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

It&#8217;s a popular music-making software (AKA DAW) used by beginners and professionals alike. </p>



<p>But how much does Cubase cost? Well, it depends on which version you choose. Cubase comes in three different versions: <strong>Elements, Artist, and Pro</strong>. And <strong><a href="/cubase-ai/" data-type="post" data-id="12275" data-lasso-id="243172">there&#8217;s also Cubase AI </a></strong>which is a free version of the software aimed at beginners just getting started with music production. </p>



<p>Each version has more features and sounds than the one before it, and they all cost different amounts. But <strong><a href="/is-cubase-free/" data-lasso-id="243250">none of Steinberg&#8217;s main versions of Cubase are free</a></strong>. </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Cubase Elements: The Beginner&#8217;s Choice</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-full"><img decoding="async" width="750" height="409" src="/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/cubase-elements.jpg" alt="cubase elements" class="wp-image-12298" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/cubase-elements.jpg 750w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/07/cubase-elements-600x327.jpg 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/07/cubase-elements-300x164.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 750px) 100vw, 750px" /></figure>



<p><a href="/what-is-cubase/" data-lasso-id="251756">Cubase Elements</a> is the simplest version of Cubase. It&#8217;s perfect for those who are just starting out in music production. Priced at $99.99, it&#8217;s an affordable way to get your hands on a professional digital audio workstation.</p>



<p>With Cubase Elements, you can work with up to 48 music tracks and 24 instrument tracks. This is more than enough for beginners and even some intermediate producers. You can record your vocals or instruments, create beats, and arrange your song all within the software.</p>



<p>Cubase Elements comes with over 1000 instrument sounds. These include drums, pianos, synths, and more. You can use these sounds to create your music without needing any additional software. </p>



<p>It also includes 45 special sound effects, or VST plugins, that you can use to enhance your tracks. These include reverb to add space to your sound, delay to create echo effects, and EQ to balance the frequencies in your mix.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Cubase Artist: The Next Step</h2>



<p>Once you&#8217;ve mastered the basics with Cubase Elements, you might find yourself wanting more. That&#8217;s where Cubase Artist comes in. This version is a bit more advanced and costs $239.</p>



<p>Cubase Artist lets you work with unlimited music tracks and instrument tracks. This means you can record as many parts as you want, from multiple vocal layers to a full orchestra. The only limit is your computer&#8217;s processing power.</p>



<p>Cubase Artist comes with over 2600 instrument sounds. This gives you even more options for creating your music. It also includes 77 special sound effects. These additional plugins can help you achieve a more professional sound.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Cubase Pro: The Professional&#8217;s Choice</h2>



<p>For those who want the best of the best, there&#8217;s Cubase Pro. This is the most advanced version of Cubase and it costs $579. It&#8217;s used by professional producers and recording studios around the world.</p>



<p>Like Cubase Artist, Cubase Pro lets you work with unlimited music tracks and instrument tracks. But it also comes with even more features. These include advanced mixing tools, surround sound support, and score editing tools for composing music.</p>



<p>Cubase Pro comes with over 3000 instrument sounds and 99 special sound effects. This vast library of sounds and effects gives you all the tools you need to create professional-quality music.</p>
