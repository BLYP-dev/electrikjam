<p>If you&#8217;re an musician looking to release your music, you&#8217;ve probably heard that mastering is an important step in the process. But what exactly is mastering, and how much does it cost?</p>



<p>First off, let&#8217;s define what mastering is. <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/audio-mastering/" data-type="post" data-id="14779" data-lasso-id="264438">Mastering is the final stage of audio post-production</a></strong>, where a mastering engineer optimizes the final mix of a song for distribution. </p>



<p>This involves adjusting levels, applying EQ and compression, and creating a cohesive sound that translates well across all playback systems.</p>



<p>So why is mastering so important? Well, have you ever listened to a professionally released track and then played one of your own bedroom-produced songs right after? </p>



<p>The difference is usually pretty stark. Mastered tracks sound louder, clearer, and more polished. They just sound&#8230; better.</p>



<p>But here&#8217;s the thing: <strong>mastering is an art form, and like any art form, it takes skill, experience, and the right tools to do it well</strong>. That&#8217;s where mastering engineers come in.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Music Mastering Prices Per Track &amp; Full Albums</strong></h2>



<p>Now, let&#8217;s talk about cost. </p>



<p>Mastering can be expensive, there&#8217;s no way around it. Rates vary depending on the engineer and their location, but you can expect to pay anywhere from $50 to $200 per song, or $500 to $2,000 for a 10-track album.</p>



<p>If you want to get your song mixed and mastered together, that&#8217;ll cost you even more &#8211; anywhere from $150 to $700 per song, or $1,500 to $7,000 for a 10-track album.</p>



<p>Factors that can influence the cost include the complexity of the song, the time required to master it, and whether you want it mastered in stereo or stems. </p>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Music-Mastering-Cost-1024x683.png" alt="Music Mastering Cost" class="wp-image-14789" title="" srcset="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Music-Mastering-Cost-1024x683.png 1024w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Music-Mastering-Cost-600x400.png 600w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Music-Mastering-Cost-300x200.png 300w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Music-Mastering-Cost-768x512.png 768w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Music-Mastering-Cost.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>Some engineers may also charge extra for additional services like digital noise reduction, editing, importing new tracks, and MP3 conversion.</p>



<p>I’ve worked as a professional mastering engineer for almost 20 years now. My journey started when my band first got signed and I was looking at ways to supplement my income –&nbsp;being in a metal band seldom pays the bills.&nbsp;</p>



<p>I worked in studios in LA and Atlanta with a huge range of artists, from KYLESA to Baroness to Katy Perry (she’s lovely, by the way), and gradually started to learn how to do audio mastering.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Over the course of the next decade and half, I honed my skills, learned from some of the best audio mastering engineers in LA, and earlier this year I took my entire process –&nbsp;the exact process I use to professionally master music –&nbsp;and put it into a framework that anyone can follow.&nbsp;</p>



<p>And the best part? You don’t need any experience software or tools. I designed the course so you can do it all using free tools like Reaper (although I have included some paid-for plugins, if you want them).&nbsp;</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Why Mastering Your Music Is Worth It</strong></h2>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li>Mastering ensures your songs sound professional and optimized across all playback devices and platforms. You don&#8217;t want your track to sound great in your headphones but like garbage on a car stereo or phone speaker.</li>



<li>Mastering engineers provide an objective set of ears. They can catch mistakes and make adjustments that you might miss after hearing your own track hundreds of times.</li>



<li>Mastering is a developed skill that takes years to perfect. Unless you&#8217;re willing to put in the time and effort to learn it yourself (which I highly recommend, by the way), it&#8217;s best to leave it to the pros.</li>



<li>Mastering can fix a lot of issues that might be present in your mix, like clicks, pops, and inconsistencies between tracks on an album.</li>
</ol>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Learn To Master Your Own Music</strong></h2>



<p>Now, you might be thinking, &#8220;Can&#8217;t I just master my own tracks?&#8221; And sure, you can. But it&#8217;s a lot harder than it sounds. Mastering your own music requires a level of impartiality that can be hard to achieve when you&#8217;re emotionally invested in the project.</p>



<p>Plus, professional mastering engineers have years of experience and access to high-end equipment that the average bedroom producer simply doesn&#8217;t have.</p>



<p>In terms of turnaround time, an experienced engineer can master a song in 10-20 minutes. But if you&#8217;re just starting out, it might take you an hour or more per track. For a full album, expect to wait a few days to a week to get your mastered tracks back.</p>



<p>Or, you can save yourself a few thousand bucks, <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/product/mastering-101-the-complete-beginner-framework/" data-lasso-id="265759">buy my Mastering 101 Framework</a></strong>, and learn to do it yourself. </p>



<p>And once you&#8217;ve been through it 15 or 20 times, you&#8217;ll be good enough to charge for your own mastering services. </p>



<p>This is a seriously bankable skill, so if you want to &#8220;do music for a living&#8221; learning a skill like mastering is one of the best ways to do it. </p>

<p>So, is professional mastering worth the cost? I say yes. Releasing music with subpar sound quality can actually do more harm than good &#8211; listeners might be turned off by tracks that sound amateur or unpolished. </p>



<p>Investing in professional mastering ensures that your music sounds its absolute best and has the best chance of success.</p>



<p>Of course, the cost of mastering can vary based on the reputation and experience of the engineer, as well as the quality of their equipment. </p>



<p>When choosing a mastering engineer, be sure to do your research &#8211; look for someone with experience in your genre and a solid track record of success.</p>



<p>One last thing to keep in mind: <strong>mastering engineers do not receive royalties for their work</strong>. They are paid a one-time fee for their services. So while the upfront cost may seem steep, it&#8217;s a one-and-done expense.</p>

<p><strong>But in my opinion, it&#8217;s worth every penny. Professional mastering ensures that your music sounds its best and has the best chance of success in today&#8217;s competitive music market.</strong></p>
