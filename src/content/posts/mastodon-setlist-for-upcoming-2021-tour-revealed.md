<p><strong>Mastodon is about to kick off a US tour with Opeth. And the setlist has apparently been leaked online…</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator has-css-opacity"/>



<p><strong><a href="/new-mastodon-album-2021-release-date-track-list-artwork-detailed/" data-type="post" data-id="3981" data-lasso-id="18349">Mastodon’s new album</a></strong>, Hushed and Grim, is a masterpiece and, perhaps, one of Mastodon’s finest records to date. I say, perhaps, but it is quickly becoming my favorite Mastodon record. Period. And I’m a big fan of the band’s earlier records like <strong>Blood Mountain</strong> and <strong>Leviathan</strong>.</p>



<p>Either way, if you’re interested in my thoughts on the new record, check out the full <strong><a href="/mastodon-hushed-and-grim-review/" data-type="post" data-id="5124" data-lasso-id="18350">Hushed and Grim review</a></strong> for a full breakdown of what makes the album tick, standout performances, and how it compares to 2017’s <strong>Emperor of Sand.</strong></p>



<p>As of today, Mastodon is now officially on tour in the USA. And it is taking none other than OPETH with it. This will be one hell of a tour. And if you’re going to see Mastodon, you might be wondering what the setlist will look like?</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Mastodon Tour Setlist</strong></h2>



<p>A leaked screenshot, claiming to be the setlist for Mastodon’s upcoming USA tour, appeared online over the weekend. </p>



<p>According to the leak, the Mastodon set will constant of 14 songs taken from the band’s entire back catalog – there are tracks from <strong>Remission</strong>, <strong>Crack The Skye, Hushed and Grim</strong>, and also <strong>Leviathan</strong>, and <strong>Blood Mountain.</strong></p>



<p>Here’s the full setlist for Mastodon’s 2021 US tour:</p>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Pain With An Anchor</strong></li>



<li><strong>Crystal Skull</strong></li>



<li><strong>Megaloden</strong></li>



<li><strong>The Crux</strong></li>



<li><strong>Teardrinker</strong></li>



<li><strong>Bladecatcher</strong></li>



<li><strong>Black Tongue</strong></li>



<li><strong>Skeleton of Splendor</strong></li>



<li><strong>The Czar</strong></li>



<li><strong>Pushing The Tides</strong></li>



<li><strong>More Than I Could Chew</strong></li>



<li><strong>Mother Puncher</strong></li>



<li><strong>Gigantium</strong></li>



<li><strong>Blood and Thunder</strong></li>
</ol>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Mastodon &amp; OPETH Tour</strong></h2>



<p>As you can see, that is a monster of a setlist.
