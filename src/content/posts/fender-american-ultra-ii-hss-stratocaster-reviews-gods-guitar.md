<p>Fender builds the American Ultra II for speed and comfort. The body is typically <strong>Alder</strong> (or Ash on certain finishes), featuring <strong>deep, sculpted rear body contours</strong> and a <strong>tapered neck heel</strong>. </p>



<p>These contours drastically improve access to the high frets and make the guitar feel incredibly ergonomic, a significant step up from the already comfortable American Pro II.</p>



<p>The finish is a flawless <strong>gloss polyurethane</strong> that provides durable protection.</p>



<p>The neck is Maple with a sleek, satin finish on the back for rapid movement. </p>



<p>The key features are the <strong>Ultra &#8220;D&#8221; neck profile</strong> and the <strong>compound radius fingerboard</strong> (10&#8243; to 14&#8243;). </p>



<p>The neck profile fills the hand comfortably for chording low down, then thins out towards the body, while the compound radius allows for effortless string bending without fretting out higher up.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="htoc-pickup-variants-breakdown-hss-power-with-zero-noise">Pickup Variants Breakdown: HSS Power with Zero Noise</h2>



<p>The HSS (Humbucker-Single-Single) configuration is what makes this guitar the ultimate do-it-all setup. Wanna go heavy? Sorted. Light and breezy? Done. Funk? You got it. Down-tuning chug? Yep. </p>



<p>This is why Strats are so damn popular. They can do anything and create any sound. With this HSS configuration, you&#8217;re basically good to go with any genre at any time. </p>



<p>For producers or bedroom recording artists, this guitar can (and will) do the work of several which means less money spent on hardware. This is why I don&#8217;t mind the pricing on this model too much; it&#8217;ll do the job of three or four guitars with ease. </p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li id="htoc-ultra-double-tap-humbucker-bridge-this-pickup-is-the-heart-of-the-versatility-it-provides-a-hot-powerful-bridge-tone-for-rock-and-metal-critically-the-double-tap-technology-delivers-a-true-articulate-single-coil-tone-when-split-via-the-s-1-switch-unlike-older-humbuckers-which-often-sound-thin-when-split"><strong>Ultra Double Tap Humbucker (Bridge):</strong> This pickup is the heart of the versatility. It provides a hot, powerful bridge tone for rock and metal. Critically, the <strong>Double Tap technology</strong> delivers a true, articulate single-coil tone when split via the S-1 switch, unlike older humbuckers which often sound thin when split.</li>



<li id="htoc-ultra-noiseless-hot-strat-single-coils-middle-neck-these-pickups-provide-genuine-vintage-strat-sparkle-chime-and-quack-but-are-completely-hum-free-this-is-a-major-advantage-for-high-gain-or-recording-situations-where-noise-is-unacceptable"><strong>Ultra Noiseless Hot Strat Single Coils (Middle/Neck):</strong> These pickups provide genuine vintage Strat sparkle, chime, and quack, but are <strong>completely hum-free</strong>. This is a major advantage for high-gain or recording situations where noise is unacceptable.</li>



<li id="htoc-tonal-versatility-the-combination-delivers-classic-glassy-strat-tones-coil-split-neck-middle-and-humbucker-roar-full-bridge-making-it-capable-of-covering-90-of-all-popular-music-genres"><strong>Tonal Versatility:</strong> The combination delivers classic glassy Strat tones (coil-split/neck/middle) and humbucker roar (full bridge), making it capable of covering 90% of all popular music genres.</li>



<li id="htoc-verdict-this-setup-sacrifices-absolutely-nothing-you-get-vintage-chime-modern-power-and-zero-noise"><strong>Verdict:</strong> This setup sacrifices absolutely nothing; you get vintage chime, modern power, and zero noise.</li>
</ul>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="htoc-tone-amp-sound-character-pristine-and-punchy">Tone &amp; Sound Character: Pristine and Punchy</h2>



<ul class="wp-block-list is-style-list-check">
<li id="htoc-clean-the-ultra-noiseless-pickups-yield-pristine-bell-like-cleans-with-crystal-clarity-and-zero-background-hum-the-middle-position-and-the-split-bridge-pickup-deliver-the-classic-strat-quack-but-with-enhanced-articulation"><strong>Clean:</strong> The Ultra Noiseless pickups yield pristine, bell-like cleans with crystal clarity and zero background hum. <p>The Ultra II HSS delivers a clean, modern, high-fidelity sound as you&#8217;d expect but the thing that&#8217;ll impress you the most, especially when paired with a decent amp, is the huge dynamics it can create. </p>



<p>I&#8217;m a bit of a bonehead player. I like big riffs and melodic, almost folksy intricate parts that are simple but build and rely on feel and atmosphere. This guitar, way more so than my PRS SE 24 Standard, can do this type of thing so well. </p>
