<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity is-style-dots"/>



<p>The guitar industry has witnessed significant changes over the past two decades, with various brands vying for the attention of musicians and enthusiasts worldwide. </p>



<p>This report examines the search interest trends for five prominent guitar brands &#8211; <strong>Gibson, Fender, Ibanez, PRS, and Epiphone</strong> &#8211; from 2004 to 2024, providing insights into the evolving preferences of guitarists and the dynamic nature of the industry.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="The_Dominance_of_Gibson_and_Fender_-"><strong>The Dominance of Gibson and Fender (2004-2009)</strong></h3>

<p>In the early years, Gibson and Fender emerged as the clear leaders in search interest, with Gibson experiencing a remarkable spike in February 2004, reaching a search interest value of 100. </p>



<p>Fender consistently maintained a strong presence, with search interest values hovering around 30-40 throughout this period. </p>



<p>Ibanez, PRS, and Epiphone remained relatively stable, with search interest values in the range of 10-20, 4-5, and 7-10, respectively.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Key Observations:</strong></h3>



<p><strong>Trends Over Time:</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Gibson</strong> shows relatively high peaks and troughs, indicating strong fluctuations in search interest. It had significant spikes around mid-2004, early 2008, and early 2013. </li>



<li><strong>Fender</strong>, similar to Gibson, also shows variability but with a more stable overall pattern. There are noticeable peaks, such as in mid-2005, late 2009, and mid-2015</li>



<li><strong>Ibanez</strong> and <strong>PRS</strong> display less dramatic fluctuations compared to Gibson and Fender. Their search interest trends are relatively more subdued, with <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/prs-se-series-guitars-guide/" data-type="post" data-id="12961" data-lasso-id="265410">PRS showing gradual increases</a> in interest over the years, particularly noticeable post-2012</strong>.</li>
</ul>



<p><strong>Comparative Analysis</strong>:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Gibson</strong> and <strong>Fender</strong> consistently dominate in terms of search interest, which could suggest stronger brand recognition or a larger fan base. Their peaks often overshadow those of the other brands.</li>



<li><strong>Ibanez</strong>, <strong>PRS</strong>, and <strong>Epiphone</strong> have lower overall search interest, but the steady interest in PRS suggests a solid and growing niche market.</li>



<li><strong>Epiphone</strong>, often considered a more budget-friendly alternative to Gibson, shows the least variability and maintains a low but steady interest level.</li>
</ul>
</div></div>



<p>The period from 2010 to 2015 saw some intriguing fluctuations in search interest for the guitar brands. </p>



<p>Gibson experienced a significant spike in July 2010, reaching a search interest value of 74, possibly due to a notable product release or a major event. </p>



<p>However, overall search interest for Gibson and Fender began to decline gradually during this period, while Ibanez, PRS, and Epiphone remained relatively stable.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="Stabilization_and_Convergence_-"><strong>Stabilization and Convergence (2016-2020)</strong></h3>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="722" src="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/popularity-of-guitar-brands--1024x722.png" alt="" class="wp-image-14932" title="" srcset="https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/popularity-of-guitar-brands--1024x722.png 1024w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/popularity-of-guitar-brands--600x423.png 600w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/popularity-of-guitar-brands--300x212.png 300w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/popularity-of-guitar-brands--768x542.png 768w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/popularity-of-guitar-brands--1536x1083.png 1536w, https://www.electrikjam.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/popularity-of-guitar-brands-.png 1699w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>From 2016 to 2020, the search interest for all five guitar brands began to stabilize and converge. </p>



<p>Gibson and Fender continued to lead the pack, with search interest values ranging from 17-29 and 16-19, respectively. </p>



<p>Ibanez, PRS, and Epiphone maintained their modest but consistent search interest levels, with values around 4-6, 3-4, and 3-4, respectively. </p>



<p>This period marked a more balanced and less volatile phase in the guitar industry.</p>

<p>The analysis of search interest trends for guitar brands from 2004 to 2024 reveals a dynamic and evolving landscape. </p>



<p>Gibson and Fender have consistently dominated the market, with occasional fluctuations and notable spikes in interest. </p>



<p>Ibanez, PRS, and Epiphone have carved out their niche positions, maintaining a stable presence throughout the years.</p>



<p>The dataset used in this report is based on global Google searches, so while it is a fairly large dataset, it isn’t perfect –&nbsp;no dataset ever is. The idea here, rather than to show all brands, was to focus on the macro trends for the Big Five Guitar Brands over the past two decades.&nbsp;</p>



<p>It does not include information about other, smaller boutique brands like Schecter, Charvel, Harley Benton, Electrical Guitar Company, and a host of other notable guitar brands that are now also popular options.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Legacy brands will always hoover-up the vast majority of search, as these are the brands that most –&nbsp;rightly or wrongly –&nbsp;strongly associate with guitar. Like when you think of a tablet, most people think of Apple and its iPad.&nbsp;</p>



<p>With guitars, when you think of an electric guitar –&nbsp;if you’re not a complete gear-head –&nbsp;most people will think of either Gibson or Fender.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Personally, we’re much more interested in smaller, more boutique brands here at ELECTRIKJAM. </p>



<p>Schecter is perhaps one of our biggest loves of recent years and, for the more budget conscious and/or beginner, brands like Squier and Harley Benton are really raising the bar with you’d expect from a “budget” beginner’s guitar. </p>
