<p>Most budget guitars are made in China and Indonesia. Major brands like Squier, Harley Benton, and Epiphone all use factories in these two countries. These factories use CNC machines that make guitar production much faster, and this drives the cost down.</p>



<p>South Korea, Japan, and the United States also use CNC machines, but the &#8220;hands-on&#8221; experience is more involved. More parts are sanded and glued by hand, and higher quality components are used to build the guitar. These factories also use better quality woods, and offer special &#8220;exotic&#8221; woods.</p>



<p>So brands and country or origin matters the most when you are wondering &#8220;what is the average cost of an electric guitar?&#8221; and it is the first thing you should consider. You definitely get what you pay for, and sticking with a well known brand is the best option.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">General Upkeep And Maintenance</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image aligncenter size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="683" src="/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Average-guitar-cost-1024x683.png" alt="What Is The Average Cost Of An Electric Guitar? " class="wp-image-10822" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Average-guitar-cost-1024x683.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Average-guitar-cost-600x400.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Average-guitar-cost-300x200.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Average-guitar-cost-768x512.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Average-guitar-cost.png 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>This is a big one that many people forget about when they buy an electric guitar. Most electric guitars, even the expensive ones, need a setup right out of the box. <strong><a href="/schecter-demon-6-worth-it-specs-price/" target="_blank" data-type="post" data-id="5301" rel="noopener" data-lasso-id="217640">Some companies like Schecter Guitars</a></strong> are &#8220;Set Up To Play&#8221; by the company before the guitar ships out to retailers.</p>



<p>This is a good practice, but not every company spends the time to make sure the guitar is playable. It can be easy to learn how to maintain your guitar at home with a few tools. But a complete beginner will probably need some help from a technician.</p>



<p>A good guitar setup usually costs 30-80 dollars depending on where you live, or the place that you take your guitar for maintenance. You should also factor in the price for strings. Strings vary in price depending on the brand, but most strings cost about $8 on average.</p>



<p>The frequency of maintenance and string changes will vary depending on the player. But it is definitely something to consider when buying an electric guitar. <strong>Most guitars will need a setup right out of the box, especially the budget brands.</strong></p>
