We have a lot to look forward to, as there are some very cool new models, and even some old ones!</strong></p>

<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="870" height="269" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/RGD70ALNB_EEM_1P_01.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1683" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/RGD70ALNB_EEM_1P_01.png 870w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/RGD70ALNB_EEM_1P_01-600x186.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/RGD70ALNB_EEM_1P_01-300x93.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/RGD70ALNB_EEM_1P_01-768x237.png 768w" sizes="(max-width: 870px) 100vw, 870px" /></figure>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Ibanez 2021 Lineup: It&#8217;s all Virtual This Year&#8230;</h2>



<p>Due to Covid 19, it seems like we are all going to be getting our usual NAAM information from articles just like the one your are currently reading. It&#8217;s not possible to hold the usual Winter trade show this year, but that doesn&#8217;t mean that companies like Ibanez are not making some great new guitars!</p>



<p>Ibanez is almost synonymous with Metal guitar players, and they frequently make our own <a href="/best-guitars-for-metal/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="597">&#8220;best of&#8221; lists.</a></p>



<p>Ibanez always has an interesting approach to Winter NAAM, usually dumping tons of new models. This year is no different, and we have some real contenders in every price tier!</p>



<p>Lets take a look at the new Ibanez 2021 Lineup!</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Signature Series: Paul Gilbert</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="870" height="288" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/PGMM11_JB_5A_01.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1684" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/PGMM11_JB_5A_01.png 870w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/PGMM11_JB_5A_01-600x199.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/PGMM11_JB_5A_01-300x99.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/PGMM11_JB_5A_01-768x254.png 768w" sizes="(max-width: 870px) 100vw, 870px" /><figcaption>PGM-11</figcaption></figure>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="870" height="290" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/FRM300_PR_4L_01.png" alt="Ibanez 2021 lineup" class="wp-image-1681" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/FRM300_PR_4L_01.png 870w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/FRM300_PR_4L_01-600x200.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/FRM300_PR_4L_01-300x100.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/FRM300_PR_4L_01-768x256.png 768w" sizes="(max-width: 870px) 100vw, 870px" /><figcaption>FRM300</figcaption></figure>



<p>This year we get not one, but two new Paul Gilbert models! However, they are not so much &#8220;new&#8221; as they are slightly re-worked. These are definitely models that we are familiar with, but with brand new finishes.</p>



<p>The PGM-11 model is the classic Paul Gilbert signature, and is a reissue from the original model from the 90&#8217;s.  This is an affordable version of the classic Gilbert model.</p>



<p>The FRM300 is the legendary &#8220;Fireman&#8221; signature guitar. This is a fully spec&#8217;d out Paul Gilbert model that features:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>Ebony Fretboard</strong></li><li><strong>Okoume Body Wood</strong></li><li><strong>Custom Dimarzio Pickups</strong></li><li><strong>Graph-Tech Nut</strong></li><li><strong>Gibraltar Bridge</strong></li><li><strong>Cream Binding</strong></li><li><strong>5 Way Super Switch</strong></li><li><strong>Narrow/Tall Frets</strong></li></ul>



<p>The Fireman has always stood out as being a rare bird for Ibanez. It is built to Paul&#8217;s specs and has all premium materials. The Custom Dimarzio pickups are Paul Gilbert&#8217;s newest design, and can be coil split or pushed out of phase.</p>



<p>The Fireman neck carve has been slightly altered as well, as it is slimmer and features narrow/Tall frets over the usual jumbo frets. But this is just the tip of the iceberg for the Ibanez 2021 lineup!</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Signature Series: Martin Miller 7 String</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="870" height="278" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/MM7_TAB_00_01.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1685" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/MM7_TAB_00_01.png 870w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/MM7_TAB_00_01-600x192.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/MM7_TAB_00_01-300x96.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/MM7_TAB_00_01-768x245.png 768w" sizes="(max-width: 870px) 100vw, 870px" /><figcaption>MM7</figcaption></figure>



<p>Martin Miller is a solo artist, new to the Ibanez Signature series. He has played with everyone in the industry at this point, and having his own signature guitar was way overdue. The MM7 features:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>AZ7 Oval CS-TECH WOOD 1pc Roasted Maple neck</strong></li><li><strong>Okoume body</strong></li><li><strong>Bound Ebony fretboardWhite dot inlay</strong></li><li><strong>Jumbo Stainless Steel fretsPrestige fret edge treatment</strong></li><li><strong>Gotoh® T1872S bridge</strong></li><li><strong>Seymour Duncan® Hyperion™7 Pickups</strong></li><li><strong>Graph Tech® BLACK TUSQ XL® nut</strong></li></ul>



<p>This Made In Japan beauty comes with all the bells and whistles. The goth bridge and locking tuners will be great for tuning stability, even with big dives.</p>



<p>The Alter Switch is a new feature, and it basically puts the pickups into a coil split/out of phase mode. Usually this is done with a knob that has a push/pull feature. But Ibanez has made it much easier with the flick of a switch. </p>



<p>This is my personal favorite from the Ibanez 2021 Lineup, at least&#8230; so far.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Signature Series: Paul Waggoner</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="870" height="292" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/PWM20_1P_01.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1686" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/PWM20_1P_01.png 870w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/PWM20_1P_01-600x201.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/PWM20_1P_01-300x101.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/PWM20_1P_01-768x258.png 768w" sizes="(max-width: 870px) 100vw, 870px" /><figcaption>PWM20</figcaption></figure>



<p>The PWM20 is Paul Waggoner&#8217;s main axe this year. The guitar virtuoso from the <a href="/90s-metal-bands/" data-lasso-id="2795">popular Metal band  Between The Buried And Me</a>  takes the Ibanez &#8220;S&#8221; shape and adds some of his own personal touches.</p>



<p>The PWM20 comes with a set of Mojotone Hornet pickups, Jumbo frets, and an Edge Zero II bridge. The model currently only comes in a see through white finish.</p>



<p>You can check out all of the signature series specs at <a href="https://www.ibanez.com/usa/news/detail/20201203155245.html" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="598" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">The Official Ibanez website.</a></p>

<p>The RG 5107 is the top of the line Prestige Series guitar. This is the most expensive on the list of the Ibanez 2021 lineup, and it has the features to show it:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li><strong>Super Wizard HP5pc Maple/ Wenge neck</strong></li><li><strong>Basswood body</strong></li><li><strong>Bound Macassar Ebony fretboard/ Off-set Mother of Pearl dot inlay</strong></li><li><strong>Gibraltar Performer bridge/ Lo-Pro Edge bridge</strong></li><li><strong>Fishman Fluence Modern Humbuckers</strong></li><li><strong>Fishman Fluence Single Coil (Middle)</strong></li><li><strong>Gold Hardware</strong></li><li><strong>Jumbo Stainless Steel frets/ Prestige fret edge treatment</strong></li></ul>



<p><a href="/fishman-fluence-modern-review-is-the-hype-legit/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="599">Fishman Fluence Modern</a> pickups have really taken off over the past year, so it&#8217;s no surprise to see Ibanez jumping on the bandwagon. This Top of the line model came with EMG pickups at one time.</p>



<p>This is a beautiful addition to the Ibanez 2021 lineup, and is the symbol of quality that the Japanese factory can produce.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The New RGA Series</h2>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="870" height="282" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/RGA61ALN_OEM_1P_01.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1694" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/RGA61ALN_OEM_1P_01.png 870w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/RGA61ALN_OEM_1P_01-600x194.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/RGA61ALN_OEM_1P_01-300x97.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/01/RGA61ALN_OEM_1P_01-768x249.png 768w" sizes="(max-width: 870px) 100vw, 870px" /><figcaption>RGA61ALN</figcaption></figure>



<p>The new RGA Series kicks off with the RGA61ALN, a the newest member of the AXION series. This metal machine comes with some crazy features that make the AXION gig-ready the day you unbox it.</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list"><li>Nitro Wizard&nbsp;5pc Panga Panga/ Walnut neck</li><li><strong>Nyatoh body</strong></li><li><strong>Macassar Ebony fretboard/ White Step off-set dot inlay</strong></li><li><strong>Jumbo Sub Zero treated frets</strong></li><li><strong>Gibraltar Standard II bridge</strong></li><li><strong>DiMarzio® Fusion Edge Pickups</strong></li><li><strong>Luminlay Side Dots</strong></li><li><strong>Schaller Strap Locks</strong></li></ul>



<p>Since we have seen it on the RG series, lets talk about the &#8220;Sub Zero Fret Treatment&#8221;. This is a practice started by Gibson guitars, and it looks like the AXION label has taken it up as their own.</p>



<p>These are cryogenically treated frets. So while they are still your standard Nickle fretwire, it lasts 30% longer. This is great to see on the <a href="/ibanez-guitars/" data-lasso-id="255021">more affordable models of the Ibanez 2021 Lineup</a>.</p>
