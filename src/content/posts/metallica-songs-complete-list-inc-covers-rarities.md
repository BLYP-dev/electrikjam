<p><strong>Every single Metallica song ever recorded, including covers, rarities, and lesser-known tracks as well as all the bangers we know and love from Metallica&#8217;s back catalog in one place&#8230; </strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator is-style-wide"/>



<p>Formed in 1981, Metallica is perhaps one of the most well known bands on the planet. <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/the-guitar-james-hetfield-could-not-live-without/" data-lasso-id="91764">James Hetfield</a> and the boys have been kicking out albums every few years or so since 1984 –&nbsp;almost 40 years ago!</p>



<p>As pioneers of thrash metal, Metallica, powered in part by <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/what-guitar-pick-does-james-hetfield-use/" data-type="post" data-id="2103" data-lasso-id="1560">James Hetfield’s incredible right-hand</a></strong>, have done more for modern metal music than any other band in existence (save for <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/doom-metal-beginners-guide/" data-type="post" data-id="1042" data-lasso-id="1561">Black Sabbath</a></strong>). The fact they’re still recording and touring is a testament to the band’s enduring appeal.</p>



<p>Over the years, <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/new-metallica-album-release-date-new-songs-update/" data-lasso-id="17569">Metallica has recorded 10 studio albums</a>, performed in nearly every country on earth, and sold tens of millions of albums, a fact that has made all of the members millionaires ten times over.</p>



<p>How many songs has Metallica recorded to date? Quite a few, especially if you include its extensive back catalog of covers and collaborations – like the album it did with Lou Reed or its S&amp;M albums.</p>



<p>Here’s a complete list of Metallica songs, including the album they appeared on, writing credits for the track, and the year the song came out.</p>
