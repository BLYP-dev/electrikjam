<p><strong>The Boss Katana 50 is an extremely popular amp. Stage-ready with 50W of power, this insanely clever and useful guitar amp has everything you need to create huge sounding tones. But is it worth it? Let’s find out</strong></p>



<hr class="wp-block-separator is-style-wide"/>



<p>The Boss Katana 50 is one of those amps that you hear about a lot, and with good reason too. The Boss Katana 50 is used and abused by hundreds of thousands of players, professional and amateur alike, all over the globe.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Based in Japan, BOSS has quickly created some of the <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/the-best-guitar-amps-under-100-bucks/" data-type="post" data-id="2790" data-lasso-id="1624">best guitar amps</a> on the planet. And the <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/boss-ga-fc-ex-footswitch-new/" data-lasso-id="155685">Boss Katana</a> 50 is, arguably, one of the most popular options, thanks to its agreeable price tag and deep and rich features set. </p>



<p>With the Boss Katana 50, you literally have everything you need – from five unique amp characters, five independent effects options, support for footswitches, a super-loud 12in 50w speaker, and a built-in <a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/how-to-record-electric-guitar-at-home-guide/" data-type="post" data-id="1193" data-lasso-id="1625">recording tech</a> in the form of BOSS Tone Studio, so you can run the amp directly into your computer. </p>



<p>This means the Boss Katana 50 is great for playing live, recording, and practicing. It has all the trappings of a classic amplifier, something truly special, and like <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/killer-guitar-tone-without-hardware/" data-type="post" data-id="1924" data-lasso-id="1626">the Spark Amp from Positive Grid</a></strong>, it is focussed on smart features and connectivity with your computer for recording demos at home and in the studio. </p>

It placed really high too. And the reason is simple: whether you’re using this at home or in the studio to demo and/or practice, the amp has everything you need to get the job done.</p>
