<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>What You&#8217;ll Learn With This Guide</strong></h2>



<p>Mastering is an art-form. No one is born good at mastering. It takes years of training and a good ear – this is why people that do it professionally are referred to as mastering engineers.</p>



<p>They’re highly skilled and extremely well paid.</p>



<p>My name is Chris, and if you’re a regular at ELECTRIKJAM, you’ll know who I am and what I’m about.</p>



<p>I’ve worked in some of the top studios in LA with artists ranging from Katy Perry to KYLESA and Baroness.</p>



<p>This course is the distillation of 20+ years of experience into one, easy to follow document. </p>



<p>Think of it as a shortcut, a way to skip over all the hurdles, pitfalls, and mistakes I made when I was starting out.</p>



<p>I write, produce, and master songs professionally. Alongside ELECTRIKJAM, this is how I make a living.</p>



<p>I learned from some of the best mastering engineers in the music industry, cut my teeth working on major record albums during the late-2000s, and finally went out on my own in 2020. This guide covers everything that I learnt during this period. It is how I master tracks professional today.</p>



<p>It is literally all you need to take your songs and albums from “demo-quality” to sounding like a major record label release.</p>
