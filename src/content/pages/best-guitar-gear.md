<p>As you progress with guitar and song writing in general, you’ll naturally start gravitating towards additional, supplementary gear –&nbsp;things like <strong><a class="rank-math-link" href="https://www.electrikjam.com/category/gear/" data-lasso-id="33">effects, pedals, different types of amps</a></strong>.</p>



<p>And that’s great: <strong>this is how guitarists define their own, unique sound</strong>. Just don’t let yourself get overwhelmed with gear – it can be addictive! Focus on getting better on ACTUALLY playing guitar.</p>



<p>This is the most important thing. Gear can make a great guitarist sound incredible, but no amount of gear or effects will cover poor playing. <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/learning-guitar-guide-what-to-expect/" class="rank-math-link" data-lasso-id="34">A solid foundation comprised of fretboard knowledge</a></strong>, picking techniques, and scales is more important that having a massive pile of plugins, amps, and pedals.</p>

<h3 class="rank-math-question ">What is The Best Guitar For A Beginner? </h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>The best guitar for a beginner is one that you can afford, that plays well, and doesn’t totally suck. <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/epiphone-sg-standard-vs-epiphone-sg-custom/" class="rank-math-link">This is why we ALWAYS recommend the Epiphone SG Standard</a></strong>. Not only is it a brilliant all round guitar but it is great for beginners thanks to its lightweight design, thin neck, and amazing tone.</p>
<p>It isn’t what you’d call “cheap” but <strong><a class="rank-math-link" href="https://www.thomannmusic.com/epiphone_sg_standard_ebony.htm?offid=1&amp;affid=635" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow">it is very accessible with respect to price (less than $500)</a></strong> when compared to guitars from Fender and Gibson, and even brands like ESP and PRS. With the SG Standard, you’re getting a brilliant guitar that will grow with you as a player, so when the time comes you can play it live and record.</p>

</div>

<div id="faq-question-1618817493192" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">What Are The Best Guitar Brands? </h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>When it comes to the best guitar brands, you have the household names: Fender, Gibson, Epiphone, PRS, ESP, and Ibanez. These are the biggest and most popular guitar brands on the planet. Used by professionals and hobbyist players alike, they’re popular for a very good reason.</p>
<p>If you go with a Gibson, you’re getting a piece of history. Ditto Fender. But it will cost you a lot of money. With brands like Epiphone and Ibanez, <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/best-guitar-for-beginners/" class="rank-math-link">you have options for considerably cheaper guitars</a></strong> that are still very good with respect to overall playability, specs, and tone.</p>
<p>Bottomline? If you’re a beginner, you’ll want to be looking at guitars from Epiphone and Ibanez over guitars from Fender and Gibson. Unless you’re a millionaire, then just get whatever you want from Fender and Gibson…</p>

</div>
</div>
<div id="faq-question-1618817540474" class="rank-math-list-item">
<h3 class="rank-math-question ">What Do I Need To Get Started Playing Guitar?</h3>
<div class="rank-math-answer ">

<p>There is a lot of gear available. Too much, arguably. And this can complicate things. But all you need to get started with guitar is as follows: a guitar (a good electric one), a decent practice amp, some decent picks, and a spare set of strings. Once you’ve this, you’re good to go. You can acquire more advanced gear as you progress. If you’re just starting out, keep it simple.</p>
