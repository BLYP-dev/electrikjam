<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Why I Created This Guide</strong></h2>



<p>Mastering is an art-form. No one is born good at mastering. It takes years of training and a good ear –&nbsp;this is why people that do it professionally are referred to as <strong>mastering engineers</strong>.&nbsp;</p>



<p>They’re highly skilled and extremely well paid.&nbsp;And the reason? <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/is-it-hard-to-learn-music-mastering-yes-heres-why/" data-lasso-id="265276">Mastering music is hard work</a></strong> –&nbsp;it takes skill, knowledge, and practice.</p>



<p>My name is Chris, and if you’re a regular at ELECTRIKJAM, you’ll know who I am and what I’m about.&nbsp;</p>



<p>I’ve worked in some of the top studios in LA with artists ranging from <strong>Katy Perry to KYLESA and Baroness.&nbsp;</strong></p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img decoding="async" width="1024" height="636" src="/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Mastering-techniques-1024x636.png" alt="" class="wp-image-14686" title="" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Mastering-techniques-1024x636.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Mastering-techniques-600x373.png 600w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Mastering-techniques-300x186.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Mastering-techniques-768x477.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Mastering-techniques-1536x955.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Mastering-techniques-2048x1273.png 2048w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></figure>



<p>This course is the <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/product/mastering-101-the-complete-beginner-framework/" data-lasso-id="265756">distillation of 20+ years of experience</a></strong> into one, easy to follow document. </p>



<p>Think of it as a shortcut, a way to skip over all the hurdles, pitfalls, and mistakes I made when I was starting out.&nbsp;</p>



<p>I write, produce, and master songs professionally. Alongside ELECTRIKJAM, this is how I make a living.&nbsp;</p>



<p><strong>I learned from some of the best mastering engineers in the music industry</strong>, cut my teeth working on major record albums during the late-2000s, and finally went out on my own in 2020.&nbsp;</p>



<p>This guide covers everything that I learnt during this period. It is how I master tracks professional today. </p>



<p>It is literally all you need to <strong>take your songs and albums from “demo-quality” to sounding like a major record label release</strong>.&nbsp;</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Your Music. Transformed…</strong></h2>



<p>First impressions are everything. With music, even more so. If you want your music to stand out, it needs to be mastered correctly –&nbsp;the different it makes is night and day.&nbsp;</p>



<p>You can get your songs mastered professionally; this will cost anywhere from <strong>$1500 to $10,000</strong> depending on who you use and how many tracks you’re doing.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Or, for $35.99, you can learn to do it yourself with <strong><a href="https://www.electrikjam.com/product/mastering-101-the-complete-beginner-framework/" data-lasso-id="265757">ELECTRIKJAM&#8217;s Mastering 101: The Complete Beginner&#8217;s Guide. </a></strong></p>



<p>Not only that, but once you learn how to master like a pro, you can start charging for your skills.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Playing in a metal band doesn’t usually pay the bills, not unless you’re at a level like Mastodon, The Haunted, or Periphery.&nbsp;</p>



<p>But if you can charge $60 to $100 an hour for your mastering services, that’ll go a long ways to paying your bills, allowing you to focus more on your music and do what you love.&nbsp;</p>



<p>And it sure as shit beats working at Wendy&#8217;s too. </p>
